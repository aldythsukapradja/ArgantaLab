💡 The one decision: this concept moves the analyst charts — power-user histogram, cohort triangle, region map, device split, per-page detail — off Portfolio and into the Growth tab. That trade is what buys the breathing room. Portfolio becomes the 60-second glance; Growth stays the drill-down.

Why this reads as "pixel perfect" and the old one didn't

1Fewer, bigger, confident

The old screen crammed 7 modules in and shrank each until nothing had presence — flat gray text everywhere. This shows 5 zones, each large enough to land. Hierarchy is the fix, not smaller pixels.

2An honest funnel

Old bars were normalized to a benchmark, so "−100%" looked catastrophic and "1143%" maxed out — unreadable. New: the number is the truth, the bar just shows where you sit vs benchmark, a dot is the verdict.

3One design system

Old mixed sentence-case titles, ALL-CAPS section headers and cryptic keys ("num", "farm-perso…"). New: one type scale, one micro-label style, an 8px rhythm, humanized labels, tabular figures.

4The moat gets the stage

Measured cross-app attention is the story only Arganta can tell, so it's the centerpiece — big ranked bars + share donut — not a sliver fighting a broken-looking histogram for space.

5Context, not clutter

The old right rail crammed 5 sub-charts into one narrow column. New: one elegant who · when · where ribbon; the deep breakdowns each get real room in Growth.

6Sparse-aware by design

Day-one data stays honest ranked bars framed as "early days," never a shrunk chart pretending to be a trend. It looks intentional, not broken.
