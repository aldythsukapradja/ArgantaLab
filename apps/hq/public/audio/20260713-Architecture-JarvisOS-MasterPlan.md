## Correct partnership placement

Partnership belongs inside **Landing**. Landing explains Arganta, helps visitors discover the products, and provides a pathway for selected solo founders to build or polish apps/games, publish through KinetikCircle Apps, test with the community, run live launch activities and iterate from real signals. Planned capabilities must be labelled planned.
