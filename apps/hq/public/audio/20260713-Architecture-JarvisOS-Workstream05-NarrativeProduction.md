# Jarvis OS Workstream 05 - Narrative Production

> [!summary]
> Produce the canonical scene-by-scene screenplay and ElevenLabs/media package.

## Audio convention

`<scene>-<cue>-<voice>-<slug>.mp3`; one idea per clip; 3–10 seconds preferred; avoid volatile numbers; narration remains separate from silent product videos.

## Voice direction

JM: calm strategic adult male. KF: warm practical adult female. AB: curious polished young male. LG: warm imaginative young female. Child voices must not sound cartoonish.

## Starter acts

Act I ignition/cockpit; Act II six instruments; Act III five products; Act IV THINK/KNOW/DO; Act V architecture graph; Act VI agentic proof; Act VII return.

## Product pattern

Jarvis intro → specialist → popup Overview → desktop → mobile → specialist summary → Jarvis strategic summary. Guided replaces videos with Aldyth live takeover.

## Landing narrative

Jarvis explains Landing as public front door, product discovery and partnership pathway. Partner flow: discover → intake → build/polish → KinetikCircle Apps → community testing → live launch → signals → iteration.

## Architecture narrative

Jarvis explains Founder → Command → Vault → Data → Architecture → Agents → Products, with source visibility and approval-gated action.

## Deterministic proof

Question: Why is activation weak? THINK locates activation; KNOW reveals unwired exits; THINK selects measurement before redesign; DO builds an instrumentation package and stops at founder approval.

## Scene record

Every scene contains purpose, takeaway, speaker, exact text, voice direction, subtitle, component, data policy, animation, camera, Guided behavior, Auto behavior, media, exit, next and acceptance.

## Effort

42–74 focused days; 800k–1.8M cumulative tokens.

## Best delegation

Lead: GPT-5.6 Thinking or Opus-class. Script editor: separate reasoning/writing model. Manifest support: Codex/Sonnet.

## Acceptance

Every line is ElevenLabs-ready; every handoff is explicit; media cues are stable; Landing partnership is correctly placed; graph narration reflects real HQ architecture.
