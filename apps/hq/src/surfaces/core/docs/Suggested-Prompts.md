# Suggested Prompts

Copy, tweak, send. Each block maps to a real tool in [[Capabilities]]. Phrasing that says *make / build / generate / show* reliably triggers a tool; a plain question gets a text answer.

## Images
- `Generate an image of a floating island with a great tree, dreamy pastel sky`
- `Make a moody product hero shot of a matte-black water bottle on wet stone`
- `Create a friendly mascot: a round robot holding a diamond, flat vector style`

## Voice
- `Voice this in KF: "Welcome to Arganta — let's build something today."`
- `Read this line in JM: "Systems nominal. Standing by."`

## Websites & apps
- `Build a landing page for a coffee shop called Solstice — warm, minimal, one call-to-action`
- `Create a web app: a simple habit tracker with add, check-off, and a streak counter`
- `Make a quick landing page for a padel tournament this weekend`

## Decks
- `Make a slide deck pitching Arganta Family to guardians — problem, solution, pricing`

## Brand kits
- `Make a brand kit for "Lumen" — calm, premium, trustworthy`

## Data (your live numbers)
- `Chart this week's growth`
- `What's our weekly active vs last week?`
- `Analyze which product has the most attention this month`

## Memory
- `Search my Vault for what we decided about monetization`
- `What did we say about the rank season tuning?`

## Delegate to an office
- `Ask treasury what our runway looks like`
- `Consult operations on where activation is leaking`
- `Ask the roster who owns growth analytics`

## Publish
- `Publish that landing page` _(Core will confirm before anything goes public — see [[Publishing]])_

> [!tip] If a reply says "no live model"
> That's the honest free-tier hiccup, not a bug. Media tools (images/voice) still work because they run on a different engine — see [[Models and Cost]]. Just send again.
