01 — The core log

A week is not a list. It's a section through six kinds of energy.

Arganta reads every routine, event and moment in your circle and sorts it into six layers. Nobody tags anything. You just start seeing where the week actually went — and where it didn't.

Care

Meals, health, appointments, the school run. The load that keeps everyone standing, and the one that quietly falls on one person.

0–14 m
Mind

Homework, reading, focus work. Where attention gets spent before anyone's had a chance to choose.

14–29 m
Growth

Practice, training, the skills that compound. Small and repeated beats big and occasional — the layer thickens slowly.

29–46 m
Memory

Photos, moments, the record of what actually happened. The only layer you can't reconstruct later if you skip it.

46–58 m
Play

Padel, games, Saturday. The reason anyone shows up to the rest of it.

58–74 m
Calm

Rest, downtime, the space between everything else. Thin in most families. Arganta will tell you when it's thin in yours.

74–88 m

03 — Setup, in order

Four steps, about ten minutes.

STEP 01

Create the circle

Name it, add your people, pick who leads. A circle can be a family, a group of friends, or a class.

STEP 02

Set the rhythm

Add the routines that already happen every week. This is the part everyone skips — do it once and the rest of the year works.

STEP 03

Kids earn

They learn, they quest, they close rings. Diamonds land in their own ledger. You don't have to referee it.

STEP 04

You see the section

The six layers fill in over weeks. Thin calm, heavy care, no memory — the shape of your family, in plain view.

04 — The line we won't cross

Money can buy a cape. It can never buy an answer.

🌸

Bloom is earned

Lashira's gameplay fuel. It cannot be purchased, gifted, or transferred — not by you, not by anyone. If a kid has Bloom, they played for it.

💎

Diamonds are prestige

Earned across learning, and giftable by a guardian. They buy identity — skins, mounts, cosmetics. They never buy learning power, hints, or progress.

Gifts arrive through a guardian Gift Vault, capped per kid each month, so a present stays a present — and never becomes a shortcut past the work.
