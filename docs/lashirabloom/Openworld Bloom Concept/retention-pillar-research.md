# LashiraBloom Retention Pillar Research

Status: research/design note, no build. Created 2026-07-10.

Purpose: ground the five approved game pillars in proven mechanics while keeping LashiraBloom simple, global, kid-safe, and not pay-to-win.

## 3. Analog mechanic notes

### Lashira Keep

Useful mechanics:

- Township combines city building, farming, factories, orders, events, and decoration.
- SimCity BuildIt teaches city services, roads, items/materials, expansion, achievements, and city design challenges.
- Last War validates the long-term stronghold idea, but Lashira should avoid harsh war/pay-to-win pressure.

Design conclusion:

> Lashira Keep should be the persistent "why I come back" layer, not a complex city sim at first.

### Bloomwall Pass

Useful mechanics:

- Kingdom Rush succeeds on readable paths, set tower spots, tower upgrades, heroes, and special abilities.
- Plants vs Zombies-like clarity can help younger players understand lanes/waves quickly.
- Last War-style lane pressure can become a later submode, not a separate pillar.

Design conclusion:

> Start with one tower-defense/adventure map, then fold dungeon and warline into the same Defense pillar.

### Hearthrush Kitchen

Useful mechanics:

- Cooking Fever proves restaurant locations, time management, ingredients, appliances, and recipe variety.
- Overcooked proves prep/cook/serve station routing, time pressure, and co-op appeal.

Design conclusion:

> Cooking is the best non-combat active game for broad family retention.

### Fountain Festival

Useful mechanics:

- Royal Match uses match-3 levels to decorate/restore castle areas.
- Gardenscapes uses match-3 stars to restore garden areas and run events.
- Event/UGC-style islands keep the product fresh without making every experiment permanent.

Design conclusion:

> Fountain Festival is the experimentation valve. It prevents portal clutter.

### Emberring Arena

Useful mechanics:

- Brawl Stars validates short mobile-friendly competitive sessions and skin/showoff identity.
- Roblox arenas validate simple social competition and user-friendly spectacle.

Design conclusion:

> Keep competition contained, optional, normalized, and cosmetic-first.

## 4. Global design conclusion

Five pillars are enough:

1. Lashira Keep: daily/long-term builder.
2. Bloomwall Pass: active PvE strategy/adventure.
3. Hearthrush Kitchen: broad casual time-management.
4. Fountain Festival: puzzle/events/experiments.
5. Emberring Arena: social competition.

Everything else becomes a resource zone, submode, or seasonal event.
