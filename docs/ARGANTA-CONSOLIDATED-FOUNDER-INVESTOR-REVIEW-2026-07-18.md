# Arganta — consolidated founder and investor review

> **Decision in one sentence:** Arganta should stop presenting itself as an AI family operating system and prove one smaller promise: **a parent-led family growth ritual in which the family sees today at a glance, a child completes one short learning quest, and the parent receives credible evidence of progress.**

This is the consolidated view above the Fable audit, repository archaeology, knowledge base, Opus handoffs, current code, build verification, and live market checks as of 2026-07-18.

The tone is intentionally founder-supportive and idea-hostile. Arganta has unusually strong technical option value. It does not yet have market proof.

## 1. Executive investment view

### Bottom line

Arganta is an **exceptionally broad, technically substantive pre-product lab** built at unusual speed. It is not yet a startup in the economic sense because the repository still records zero verified external families, zero retention cohort, and zero real-money revenue. That distinction is not semantic: the value today is founder capability, reusable technology, and strategic options—not a proven customer franchise.

My recommendation is **conditional continue**, not “fund aggressively” and not “shut it down.”

- Continue because the learning engine, family data model, family UI, content infrastructure, and founder velocity form a real asset base.
- Narrow because the current narrative combines too many unproven behaviors: family organization, learning, gaming, memory, AI assistance, agentic action, creation, social publishing, and a studio operating system.
- Delay institutional fundraising because a small amount of real-user evidence will improve both the company and its financing position more than another quarter of code.
- Treat a $1B outcome as an **upside option**, not a planning assumption. Adjacent categories are large, but no evidence yet shows that this particular bundle has distribution, retention, or willingness to pay.

### Indicative financing view

The earlier detailed valuation audit's **$1.8M–$2.8M indicative pre-money range, $2.2M planning point, and $2.5M SAFE opening cap** is more defensible than the later Fable note's broader narrative re-rating. It is still a negotiation frame, not scientific fair value.

Today I would separate three concepts:

| Concept | View |
|---|---|
| Economic/fair-value planning range | **$1.8M–$2.8M pre-money** |
| Possible strategic-angel financing cap | **$2.5M–$4.0M**, only if the investor specifically values the founder and technical option set |
| Financing target after evidence | Reassess after 20–50 external active families, cohort retention, at least five real payers, and a verified trust baseline |

Do not describe repository depth as traction or replacement cost as enterprise value. A buyer pays for future cash flows and strategic control, not for the number of surfaces already built.

## 2. What the evidence says the company is

Repository archaeology shows a genuine convergence: a family shell, learning platform, persistent worlds, creator tooling, and founder cockpit share identity, circles, data, packages, and production infrastructure. That is a real architectural achievement.

The same evidence shows repeated strategic expansion:

1. family planner;
2. learning app;
3. game builder and marketplace;
4. family social layer;
5. persistent game worlds;
6. creator/media studio;
7. agentic founder operating system;
8. AI family assistant.

The correct interpretation is not “eight businesses already built.” It is **one capable laboratory that has discovered several candidate businesses but has not yet selected and proven one.**

The knowledge base is refreshingly honest about this: [the Master KB](../knowledge-base/00-MASTER-KB.md) records zero external users and [Founder Decisions](../knowledge-base/founder/founder-decisions.md) leaves the wedge unresolved. The later audits then resolve that open question in opposite directions:

- the 2026-07-13 valuation audit chooses **ArgantaLabs learning first**;
- the 2026-07-18 Fable audit chooses **Kinetik/family-assistant first**;
- the investor narrative chooses **KinetikCircle first**;
- the repository's deepest tested customer asset is the learning engine.

This contradiction is the central strategic issue. It cannot be closed by prose alone.

## 3. Battle test of the Fable audit

### Findings that survive

Fable is right on the highest-order issues:

1. **Shipping to a repository is not shipping to a user.** The strongest shared fact across the audit, KB, and archaeology remains no verified outside cohort.
2. **Effort is inverted.** HQ and internal studios consume disproportionate attention relative to the customer journey.
3. **Focus is the founder's principal controllable risk.** The commit history repeatedly expands the operated surface before market proof.
4. **Distribution and demand evidence are existential gaps.** No code asset compensates for them.
5. **The Customer Zero whiteboard story is emotionally strong.** It makes invisible family work visible and gives the product a credible human origin.
6. **The freeze is directionally correct.** HQ, forge, game-world, influencer, and studio expansion should stop unless directly required by a live pilot.
7. **The product should be glance-first, chat-second.** A family board is a daily interface; chat is best for capture, changes, explanation, and digests.
8. **Trust must be a product feature.** In a family/child context, consent, visibility, deletion, retention, and approval cannot be legal-page afterthoughts.
9. **The current valuation is evidence-constrained.** Retention and payment will re-rate the company; more internal tooling will not.

### Findings that need correction

| Fable position | Battle-tested correction |
|---|---|
| “The family-assistant wedge is correct.” | It is a plausible hypothesis, not a conclusion. An earlier audit selected learning first, and no external demand test resolves the conflict. |
| “Arganta-chat-brain is the crown jewel.” | Its architecture is a good pattern, but its only live tool in code is `generate_image`; the family data tools are declared but unwired. The visible assistant uses deterministic client-side routing and direct Supabase reads. |
| “The AI wall is the only door.” | It is the only current generative-media door, not the only family-data door. Calendar, Today, and learning reports are read directly from the browser under RLS. |
| “The wedge already contains an AI assistant.” | The current user-facing “assistant” is a keyword classifier and component router. That can be useful, but it should not be marketed as an agentic AI assistant. |
| “PWA/native is absent.” | Kinetik and ArgantaLabs already contain Capacitor paths. What is absent is a verified stranger-ready store distribution and notification journey. |
| “There is no CI.” | CI exists for shared packages, edge routers, and HQ type-checking. The real gap is that landing, Kinetik, ArgantaLabs build/test, migrations, and RLS probes are not protected by customer-path CI. |
| “$4.99/month is a plausible default.” | It is an untested assumption. Current official pricing spans Cozi Gold at $39/year, Cozi Max at $79.99/year, Hearth at $9/month, and Ollie at $25–$100/month. Price should be tested against the actual job and cost structure. |
| “40% week-4 retention” is the stage gate. | It is an aggressive heuristic without a defined cohort. Define activation and cohort first; use several behavioral and qualitative signals. |
| “Memory creates an uncopyable moat.” | Memory can improve switching costs, but only if families contribute useful data and trust the product. It also creates migration, deletion, and lock-in ethics obligations. |
| “The category supports a unicorn.” | Adjacent success proves possibility, not Arganta's probability. TAM division is not a venture case; distribution efficiency and durable retention are the missing load-bearing evidence. |

### External facts that materially change the audit

The market has moved beyond the audit's simplified competitor picture:

- [Ollie](https://ollie.ai/) already positions itself as a family AI assistant that monitors calendars and inboxes, and its [current pricing](https://try.ollie.ai/pricing/) ranges from free to $25 and $100 per month. This is stronger willingness-to-pay evidence than the audit's assumed $4.99, but it also means logistics-only differentiation is weak.
- [Cozi Max](https://www.cozi.com/cozi-max/) now includes AI event import, recipe generation, and meal planning at $79.99/year; [Cozi Gold](https://www.cozi.com/faq/) is $39/year. AI is becoming a feature inside incumbent family organization products.
- [Hearth](https://hearthdisplay.com/products/hearth-display) sells a $699-class shared display plus a $9/month membership, including an AI companion. The category signal is not merely “families pay”; it is that **ambient, glanceable household presence** can be more valuable than chat.
- Google already creates a [shared family calendar](https://support.google.com/families/answer/7157782) and supports children on shared Assistant/Gemini devices with parental controls. Platform competition is real.
- Duolingo ended 2025 with more than 50M DAU and over $1B in bookings, while its current strategy emphasizes user growth and teaching quality. This validates learning habit economics, but not a family-OS bundle. See [Duolingo's official FY2025 results](https://investors.duolingo.com/news-releases/news-release-details/duolingo-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results).

The competitive conclusion is therefore:

> **Calendar + AI is not a moat. Learning alone is not a moat. The potentially differentiated asset is the closed loop between a child's real learning effort and a parent's useful, emotionally resonant family view.**

### Specific product-truth gaps

1. [ChatApp](../apps/landing/src/chat/ChatApp.tsx) imports `answer` from the deterministic `brain.ts`, not the edge brain. It also includes an artificial 620 ms delay, making rule-based output look like model thinking.
2. [arganta-chat-brain](../supabase/functions/arganta-chat-brain/index.ts) exposes a large tool list in health output, but every action except image generation returns “unknown or not-yet-wired.” Health should distinguish `liveTools` from `plannedTools`.
3. [brainClient](../apps/landing/src/chat/brainClient.ts) is used for story-image generation, not the normal family conversation path.
4. [Drawer](../apps/landing/src/chat/Drawer.tsx) and Conversation explicitly state that history is in-memory; persistence remains planned.
5. [K1 embed handoff](arganta-chat/K1-KinetikCircle-Embed-Plan.md) states that the 65-minute refresh-token soak and real cross-origin session application are unverified.
6. Composer placeholders ask about dinner and bedtime stories while `brain.ts` explicitly refuses those jobs. This creates expectation debt during the first impression.
7. The current CI workflow protects packages and HQ, not the customer wedge. This is the reverse of the stated strategic priority.

### Product architecture ruling

For the pilot, the iframe bridge may remain if it shortens time to real families and passes the auth soak. Do not invest in polishing it as permanent architecture. After evidence, converge the retained parent surfaces into one Arganta client shell or a shared component package.

The architecture should tell the truth:

```text
Family UI data views  -> caller JWT -> Supabase RLS
AI reads/actions      -> arganta-chat-brain -> allowlisted tools -> caller JWT/RLS
Write action          -> draft -> parent approval -> execution -> audit event
Internal HQ           -> separate founder-only wall
```

Direct client reads are not inherently wrong when RLS is correct. The problem is claiming a single edge wall that the product does not actually use. All generative interpretation and all consequential writes should pass through the audited wall.

## 5. Strategic ruling: the wedge

### Four serious alternatives

| Alternative | Strongest case | Fatal risk | Ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Family logistics/assistant first | Real founder story, weekly utility, Kinetik already exists, high competitor pricing suggests willingness to pay | Crowded; requires inbox/calendar integrations and trust; current “AI” is not real | **Do not lead with generic logistics** |
| Kids learning first | Deepest tested asset; clear child action; large proven category | Crowded; educator credibility and parent acquisition are hard; can become another content game | **Use as differentiation, not a standalone app pitch** |
| School B2B2C first | Schools can aggregate distribution and validate learning | Long sales cycles, procurement, safeguarding, curriculum proof, support burden | **Use schools/communities as pilot channels, not initial buyers** |
| Agent-first | Matches founder technical advantage and future interface trends | Technology without a narrow job; safety/cost complexity; competitors/platforms commoditize agents | **Agentic capability is a later interaction layer** |

### The recommended wedge

**Target user:** a dual-income parent of a child aged roughly 7–11, initially reached through the founder's trusted Indonesian/Qatar family and school-community networks, who coordinates family life through a mix of memory, WhatsApp, calendars, and a physical board—and lacks a credible view of the child's day-to-day learning progress.

**Job:** “Help me see whether today is handled and whether my child is actually growing, without becoming the family's human database.”

**Core loop:**

1. Parent seeds the family board with the minimum necessary schedule.
2. Child completes one five-minute math/logic quest.
3. The parent board shows a specific, evidence-backed learning win.
4. A weekly reset summarizes the coming week and the child's progress.
5. Parent reacts, adjusts, or celebrates; the child sees that effort matters.

**Positioning:**

> **Arganta makes family progress visible—today's plan for parents, one small quest for kids, and real proof that the family is moving forward.**

This is one ritual, not three products. It uses the whiteboard emotion, Kinetik utility, and tested learning asset without asking the market to adopt a “family OS.”

### What not to promise yet

- a universal family AI assistant;
- autonomous agents;
- a compounding family knowledge graph;
- a marketplace or creator economy;
- a persistent family metaverse;
- curriculum efficacy;
- “un-copyable” memory;
- $1B inevitability.

## 6. Launch geography ruling

The audit treats Indonesia as a beachhead without enough evidence. Indonesia is strategically attractive but operationally specific:

- its digital economy approached $100B GMV in 2025, according to the [Google/Temasek/Bain e-Conomy SEA report](https://blog.google/intl/id-id/company-news/outreach-initiatives/e-conomy-sea-2025-ekonomi-digital-indonesia-mendekati-gmv-us100-miliar/);
- Bank Indonesia reported 14.26B digital-payment transactions in Q4 2025, up 39.21% year over year ([official monetary policy report](https://www.bi.go.id/en/publikasi/laporan/Pages/Laporan-Kebijakan-Moneter-Triwulan-IV-2025.aspx));
- Indonesia's [Personal Data Protection Law](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022%20) applies, and its [2025 child digital-protection regulation](https://peraturan.go.id/id/pp-no-17-tahun-2025) raises the bar for child-facing systems.

The correct near-term decision is **network-first, not country-first**:

1. recruit the first 10–20 families wherever founder trust and observation are strongest;
2. deliberately include Bahasa Indonesia/English code-switching in the pilot;
3. compare Indonesian and international/expat behavior rather than assuming equivalence;
4. choose Indonesia-first, global-English-first, or a diaspora wedge only after activation, payment, and trust interviews.

Do not build local payment rails before willingness to pay exists. When payment is proven, test web/card and Indonesia-native options based on the actual cohort.

## 7. Product and agentic roadmap

### The only year-one surfaces

1. **Today:** family schedule, responsibilities, and the latest credible child win.
2. **Quest:** one narrow learning domain first—math/logic—using the tested engine.
3. **Progress:** evidence, not vanity XP; what was attempted, accuracy/mastery trend, and what to try next.
4. **Weekly reset:** a generated but source-grounded summary with parent confirmation.
5. **Family controls:** members, roles, consent, visibility, export, deletion, and audit history.

Calendar and Moments can support Today, but they should not each become a roadmap universe.

### Three year-one agents, gated by evidence

| Agent | User value | Autonomy | Earliest gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly Briefing Agent | Drafts the Sunday family plan and progress summary from real rows | Read-only draft; parent edits/sends | After 10 families have manually repeated the reset twice |
| School Intake Agent | Converts a parent-forwarded email/PDF/photo into proposed events/tasks | Draft only; source attached; parent approves each write | After calendar capture is a measured activation bottleneck |
| Schedule Change Agent | Turns natural language into a proposed event/routine change | Preview diff and explicit approval before write | After RLS/write-tool tests and audit logging are green |

No autonomous booking, messaging, purchasing, publishing, or child-facing open-ended companion in year one. The agent layer earns autonomy through observed trust, not roadmap ambition.

### Technical priorities

**Now:**

- define activation and instrument it end to end;
- add customer-path CI for landing/Kinetik/ArgantaLabs;
- add auth, RLS, cross-circle denial, and write-approval tests;
- order/squash migrations into a reproducible baseline and establish staging;
- remove silent mock fallback from production customer paths;
- relabel live versus planned tools;
- remove misleading UI promises;
- verify account deletion/export and data-retention behavior;
- run the iframe token-refresh soak if the pilot still depends on it.

**After retention evidence:**

- merge retained family surfaces into one client shell;
- add import automation and push/native work based on observed behavior;
- implement the three bounded agents;
- add a simple, provenance-rich family memory table only for repeatedly requested facts.

**Not before 300 active retained families:**

- knowledge graph/vector memory;
- creator marketplace;
- new game worlds;
- generalized app builder;
- autonomous multi-agent family routines;
- large HQ expansion.

## 8. Trust is a launch gate

The architecture has strong instincts—caller JWTs, RLS, a tool allowlist, a kid denial at the edge function, and approval-gate patterns. The product and operating controls are not yet proven.

Before inviting families outside the founder's direct supervision, verify:

1. adult account creation and child linkage;
2. verifiable parent/guardian consent appropriate to the launch jurisdiction;
3. child and cross-circle access denials in automated tests;
4. data minimization and a written retention schedule;
5. parent-visible export and deletion that work end to end;
6. provider data-use and retention terms for every model/media call;
7. report/block/moderation behavior for any shared or generated content;
8. an incident-response owner and family-notification template;
9. backups and a restore drill;
10. no secret, private endpoint, child name, or family event in logs or analytics payloads.

The FTC's amended COPPA rule reinforces parental consent, deletion, data minimization, and purpose-limited retention ([FTC final rule summary](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-finalizes-changes-childrens-privacy-rule-limiting-companies-ability-monetize-kids-data)). This report is strategic advice, not a legal opinion; obtain jurisdiction-specific counsel before public child-facing launch.

## 9. Ninety-day evidence plan

The earlier 90-day plan is too feature-heavy for an unknown founder capacity. Use a capacity envelope: **20 founder hours/week assumed; 240 hours total; maximum 60% build, 25% customer work, 10% trust/ops, 5% review.** If actual availability is lower, reduce cohort size—not customer contact.

### Days 1–14 — make the test honest

Budget: ~40 hours.

- Ratify the family-growth ritual as the provisional wedge.
- Freeze new HQ/studio/world work in a visible decision note.
- Define five events: invited, activated, quest completed, parent viewed evidence, weekly reset completed.
- Remove or relabel unbuilt assistant promises and planned tools.
- Put the customer builds and critical engine tests in CI.
- Run RLS/cross-circle probes and the embed-session soak.
- Create a trust checklist and consent language for the closed pilot.
- Prepare two demand-test landing messages: logistics-led versus learning-evidence-led. The product work remains one loop; only the acquisition message varies.

### Days 15–35 — 10-family concierge pilot

Budget: ~70 hours.

- Recruit 10 families outside the household; no more than half should be close relatives.
- Observe onboarding live; measure time to first child quest and first parent evidence view.
- Run 20 parent interviews, including five non-users who decline.
- Hold weekly founder support in one group/chat channel.
- Fix only activation blockers, trust defects, and data-loss/reliability issues.

### Days 36–60 — retention and price

Budget: ~65 hours.

- Run the weekly-reset ritual for four cohorts.
- Show real price choices: $9/month or $79/year as the base test, with a higher agent-assisted concept only as a fake door.
- Ask for payment from at least 10 activated families; compliments do not count.
- Test one share/referral moment based on a parent-approved progress card.
- Compare which acquisition message produces activated families, not clicks.

### Days 61–90 — scale or cut

Budget: ~65 hours.

- If gates pass, expand to 20–50 weekly external families through one school/community partner and parent referrals.
- If gates fail, do not add features. Choose one correction: onboarding, job/positioning, or audience.
- Produce the investor evidence pack: cohort chart, five family interviews, real payment evidence, trust controls, live demo, and a one-page financial model.

### Decision gates

Define the cohort before seeing results.

| Signal | Continue/scale | Investigate | Stop or materially pivot |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Invited family activates within 48h | ≥60% | 35–59% | <35% after three onboarding iterations |
| D7 return among activated families | ≥30% | 15–29% | <15% |
| Week-4 weekly-reset completion | ≥30% | 15–29% | <15% |
| Parent views/responds to learning evidence weekly | ≥40% | 20–39% | <20% |
| Willing to pay at tested price | ≥30% stated and ≥5 real payers | useful qualitative signal | <20% stated and no payment |
| Referral behavior | ≥0.3 qualified invites per activated family | some shares | none after repeated value moments |
| New frozen surfaces built | 0 | — | Any: governance failure |

These are management thresholds for a concierge pilot, not universal market benchmarks.

## 10. Economics and pricing

The audit's $4.99 assumption is too early and probably too low for an AI-assisted family product. The market supports a broad range because products solve different jobs and carry different costs.

### Pricing experiment

- Free closed pilot, with price visible from the start.
- Test **$9/month or $79/year** for the family-growth product.
- Separately test a fake-door **$19/month assisted tier** only after the weekly briefing or intake agent is credible.
- Never meter emotionally important child progress by “AI messages.” Price the family outcome, not model tokens.

### Twelve-month scenario model

These are labeled hypotheses, not forecasts. “Active families” means families completing at least one core ritual in the last four weeks.

| Year-12 state | Conservative | Base | Upside |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Active families | 250 | 1,000 | 4,000 |
| Paying conversion | 15% | 25% | 30% |
| Payers | 38 | 250 | 1,200 |
| Realized monthly revenue per payer | $7 | $8 | $9 |
| Exit MRR | ~$266 | $2,000 | $10,800 |
| Variable product/AI cost per active family | $1.00 | $0.75 | $0.60 |
| Exit monthly contribution before people/G&A | ~$16 | $1,250 | $8,400 |

The model demonstrates the actual business question: at small scale, subscription revenue does not pay for a team. Capital efficiency matters, but founder labor, legal/privacy, support, content validation, acquisition, app-store fees, taxes, refunds, and payment fees cannot remain omitted.

Before fundraising, build a cash model with:

- founder minimum monthly support;
- actual cloud/model/storage bills;
- app-store versus web mix;
- one part-time/full-time hire scenario;
- legal/privacy/security spend;
- paid acquisition by channel;
- 18 months of runway and dilution.

## 11. Distribution

The content machinery is useful only if it produces interviews, activated families, or referrals. Measure every channel by activated retained families per founder hour and per dollar.

### Channel sequence

1. **Founder-network concierge:** schools, family friends, padel/community, Indonesian diaspora, and parent groups. Goal: observation and trust, not scale.
2. **Parent-to-parent referral:** a private, consented learning-progress card and family invite. Goal: prove native spread.
3. **School/community partnership as channel:** one teacher or community lead invites families; no enterprise contract yet.
4. **Founder-authentic content:** whiteboard story, real build decisions, parent learning—three posts/week maximum.
5. **Paid UGC test:** only after activation and D7 work organically. Start with a capped creative test; stop if a paid activated family cannot plausibly pay back within 12 months.

Do not operate separate public channels for ArgantaLab, KinetikCircle, LashiraBloom, HQ, or persona brands. One public name: **Arganta**.

## 12. Brand and emotional product

The audit correctly identifies relief and being seen as the founding emotion. Add pride without turning the child into a performance dashboard.

### Emotional moments to instrument

| Moment | Intended emotion | Behavioral evidence | Qualitative question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Today is handled | Relief | Parent opens/uses Today without editing chaos | “What felt easier this morning?” |
| Child effort becomes visible | Pride/recognition | Parent views or reacts to a real learning artifact | “Did this tell you something you did not know?” |
| Weekly reset | Calm/control | Family repeats the ritual | “What would you miss if this disappeared?” |
| Remembered detail | Feeling known | Correct sourced reminder is acted on | “Was this useful or intrusive?” |
| Family celebration | Connection | Parent/child acknowledges progress together | “Did this change the conversation at home?” |

Do not optimize child streak pressure, manipulative push frequency, or public sharing. The brand should make progress visible, not make a family feel measured.

## 13. Organization and founder operating system

Arganta's internal agent/HQ work is not worthless. It is simply ahead of the customer evidence it should govern.

### Founder-only responsibilities

- product thesis and what is frozen;
- customer interviews and observation;
- child/family trust decisions;
- final product taste;
- capital allocation and investor communication.

### Work AI can carry

- code implementation under a written acceptance test;
- test generation and regression triage;
- research collection with source labels;
- support-ticket clustering;
- weekly metric-packet drafting;
- content variants derived from approved facts.

AI agents must not substitute for parental consent, educator validation, legal review, user research, or a human's accountability for production incidents.

### Human hiring triggers

| Trigger | Hire/contract |
|---|---|
| 20 retained families and learning-content questions block adoption | Part-time educator/curriculum reviewer |
| Public pilot begins | Privacy/child-safety counsel review |
| 50 retained families and support exceeds 5 hours/week | Part-time family success/product ops |
| One organic channel repeatedly activates families | Growth operator to scale that channel—not before |
| 250+ active families and reliability load rises | Product engineer/ops generalist |

“Hire growth in month six” is not a strategy. Hire against a proven bottleneck.

## 14. What would make me invest

I would move from interested angel to conviction investor if Arganta showed:

1. 20–50 external families using the same narrow ritual;
2. cohort-defined D7 and week-4 behavior with a visible retention reason;
3. at least five unrelated paying families;
4. evidence that parent value and child value reinforce rather than compete;
5. one acquisition/referral path that does not require the founder to explain every screen;
6. an educator's scoped validation of the first learning domain;
7. tested consent, RLS isolation, export/delete, retention, and incident controls;
8. four consecutive weeks with no new frozen surface started;
9. a full-company cash model rather than an infrastructure-only model;
10. a founder narrative that says “we tested and cut” as confidently as it says “we built.”

## 15. Final consolidated view

Fable's most important insight survives: **the next unit of value is evidence, not code volume.** Its main overreach was treating one untested wedge as settled and describing planned architecture as present reality.

The current build is relevant. In fact, it is more relevant after cutting its claims:

- Kinetik is the parent-facing board;
- ArgantaLabs is the tested learning engine;
- the shared spine is the foundation;
- the assistant becomes capture, explanation, and digest;
- agents become bounded, approval-gated routines;
- HQ becomes an internal measurement cockpit;
- everything else becomes option value held in reserve.

The company should not try to beat Ollie at being Ollie, Duolingo at being Duolingo, or Hearth at selling a prettier calendar. It should test a loop those products do not naturally own:

> **A child's small act of learning becomes a useful, warm signal in the family's real day—and that signal helps the family return together.**

If families repeat and pay for that loop, Arganta has the beginning of a company and a credible path to a platform. If they do not, the founder will have learned this in 90 days while preserving the strongest assets. That is a much better outcome than spending another year completing the laboratory.

### Claims discipline

- **Inference:** a reasoned interpretation of facts; it must remain falsifiable.
