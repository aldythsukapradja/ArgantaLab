# Competitor Radar — Week 2026-W30 (2026-07-22)

## What moved (2026 H1)
Consolidation + productization of **agentic subsurface AI**. SLB stacked three moves in one quarter: NVIDIA foundation-model scale-up (Mar), S&P Global geoscience-software acquisition (Apr), and a Tela "app-store" Digital Marketplace (Jun) — turning agents into a distributed platform play, not a feature. The NOC-origin stack went commercial (AIQ/ADNOC pitching ENERGYai to US buyers). OSDU shipped M25 + a **v1.0 platform standard** that commoditizes the data plane.

## Biggest threat to our wedge
**Microsoft Azure Local "disconnected operations" (early-2026 GA)** — a fully on-prem, air-gapped control plane feeding Azure Data Manager for Energy. A hyperscaler now offers jurisdiction-locked self-hosted deployment — a head-on hit to the **sovereignty** leg of our wedge (threat 5).

## Direct-overlap flags (competitor motions touching our differentiators)
- **Sovereign/on-prem:** Azure Local disconnected ops — head-on collision. Sovereignty is now our most-contested leg.
- **Provenance/evidence:** Ikon Curate 2026.2 (surface unattached docs + AI query alongside subsurface data) — closest to our evidence-grounding, but still document-linking, not verifiable provenance.
- **Reproducible public demo:** Geoteric–SINTEF cross-vendor agentic demo at IMAGE 2026 (Houston, Aug) — same register as our Volve demo; monitor what they actually show.

## Verdict
No single competitor matched all three of our differentiators (sovereign + evidence + reproducible-demo) at once — wedge still defensible. Sharpen the **evidence/provenance** and **reproducible Volve demo** legs since sovereignty is now contested by a hyperscaler.
