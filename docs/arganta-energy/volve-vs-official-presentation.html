Methodology Maturity

The screening estimate used to be 7.6× too high. It isn't anymore.

Our screening STOIIP is a blanket oil-water-contact calculation over an unfaulted closure — deliberately a gross upper bound, not the reservoir engineering answer. Moving the contact from the raw deck baseline (3200 m) to a calibrated 3065 m collapsed the overstatement from 7.6× down to ~1×.

09 / 11
What We Haven't Fixed Yet

The recalibration didn't reach every file.

This is a real, current inconsistency in the codebase — surfaced by this review, not fixed by it.

⚠ Stale figure still live in the ATLAS catalogue

apps/energy/src/atlas/volve.ts still hard-codes STOIIP (screening) = 142.3 MMSm³ — the pre-recalibration, 7.6×-overstated figure from slide 4 — sitting next to a recovery factor of 0.54, which is actually the official RF, not what dividing cumulative oil by 142.3 would give (~7%).

Meanwhile public/wb/index.json's official/validation blocks already carry the corrected, internally-consistent pairing: 19 MMSm³ screening STOIIP against 53.7% RF.

Two Volve data sources in the same codebase currently disagree with each other. Whichever surface reads atlas/volve.ts for this field is quoting a number seven-plus percent below where the field's own reconciled data says it should be.

Also on record: a citation (C-KIEFT-MILTON, Volve Hugin Fm regional stratigraphy) in the citation vault is marked verified: N — not re-verified this session.

10 / 11
Bottom Line

Independently computed, not hand-tuned to match.

Every number on this deck came from a build-time script reading raw Equinor/Sodir files — not from typing the official answer into a config. The workbench started 7.6× off on STOIIP, found its own error, recalibrated, and now sits within ~1–2% of the regulator across STOIIP, recovery factor, cumulative oil, and GIIP. One known inconsistency remains open.

Matches official

STOIIP, RF, cum. oil and GIIP all land within ~0.4%–1.6% of the Sodir factpage after recalibration.

Brackets independent studies

Our recalibrated 19 MMSm³ sits inside the peer-published range (19.6–67.6 MMSm³), closest to the MBAL(F-12) figure.

Still open

atlas/volve.ts needs the same recalibration applied to wb/index.json — a same-day fix, not yet done.
