A learn-to-play MMORPG for kids & families

Kingdom of Kin

A real-time, kid-safe MMORPG built on top of ArgantaLab — where the maths, reading and science a child actually learns is the key that unlocks a living world of hunting, taming, castles and clan warfare. One island, six worlds, playable together from day one.

Nexus — the climb to 99 & the dragon Ragnarok — jobs & grades Pokémon — the monster is the pet Suikoden — grow your castle Total War — send your army

The whole game is one sentence, repeated deeper each season:

LearnDo real drills in ArgantaLab
→
UnlockKnowledge opens the world's gates
→
Hunt & tameFight Kin — kill for XP or befriend
→
GrowLevel your hero, grade your job, evolve your Kin
→
BuildFill your castle, raise your circle's army
→
CompeteContest the Emblem Wars, then go deeper

The genius of the fit: the thing that makes you stronger in the game is the thing a parent wants their kid doing anyway. Learning is not a mini-game bolted on — it is the power source.

04The learning bridge — the special sauce

The one question every educational game must answer: where does the learning actually happen? Our answer is three touchpoints — learning is the key, never a tax on every swing.

the gate

Rings unlock dungeons

Your daily ArgantaLab rings (real drills, done in the main app) are your Focus. Fill a world's ring and that world's dungeon & Job Trial open. Neglect a subject and its gate stays shut.

the light touch

A question to tame

To befriend a weakened Kin, you answer one quick question in the Friendship Window — the existing ArgantaLab mechanic. Capturing is a tiny, joyful learning moment.

the real test

Trials are quiz-battles

Keeper & Job Trials are true quiz-battles at the child's own year level, powered by KinQuest's existing quiz engine. To grade up from Cadet to Officer, you prove the maths.

Open decision: this 3-touchpoint model is the proposed answer, not yet signed off. It keeps moment-to-moment combat fun and action-driven while making knowledge the source of progression. If we want more learning pressure (e.g. answering to cast a skill) or less, this is the dial to turn.

05User journey — screen by screen

The pitchable walkthrough, from login to endgame. Every screen re-uses ArgantaLab systems where a reuse tag appears.

Login

Same account, one tap in

Sign in with the existing ArgantaLab login. Your identity, diamonds and Kin roster are already there. reuse: auth + diamonds

Seedling Cove — tutorial

Rats, deer, and your first horse

Land on the Cove with no class. Learn to move (joystick), attack, and capture on gentle critters. Claim a free starter mount — the "ride a horse" beat. reuse: Joystick, MountSprite

Class select — ~level 10

Choose one of six callings

Pick your class freely: Cadet, Interpreter, Medic, Researcher, Surveyor, or Technician. This sets your home city — but not where you can play.

Home city

Meet your Keeper, see your castle

One-time warp to your home city (always safe). Get your first skill and starter gear. Your home dungeon shows its level lock. reuse: Keepers Mira/Sol/…

World map

Detour to Numeria, the beginner world

Since your hometown may be high-level, you warp to Numeria — tuned for newcomers — and begin the shared journey west-to-east. Fast-travel between visited cities is free.

Hunting grounds

The monster is the Kin

Fight wild Kin for Base & Job XP — or weaken and befriend them into your roster, where they evolve as your bond grows. reuse: Kin catalog + Nexus roster

The dungeon & the trial

Beat the Keeper, change your grade

With your rings filled, the region's single dungeon opens. Its boss is a quiz-battle Job Trial — win, and grade up (Cadet → Officer → General).

The castle

Store, care, craft, upgrade

Return home to house your Kin in the Barracks, buy mounts at the Stable, heal at the Inn, and track your collection in the Archive. reuse: careKin, harvest, mount shop

The Apex — endgame

Level 99, and the dragon

All classes converge at the summit for the Council of Elders trial and a co-op raid against the corrupted Naga. Post-99, hunting becomes permanent stat gains.

Emblem Wars — social endgame

Your circle vs. the world

From the War Room, your circle raises an army from its Kin and heroes and contests a region's shrine. Glory and cosmetics only — no losses. reuse: ArgantaCup, circles

06Battle system

Kid-simple, tech-light, readable at a glance. Deliberately minimal at launch — depth is added as data, not rewrites.

Overworld combat

Tab-target, not twitch. Tap a Kin to target, auto-attack begins, and you fire up to 4 skills on cooldowns. HP & a resource bar. No aiming, no dodging skill-shots — friendly for young hands and weak devices.

Server-authoritative resolution (Colyseus) so nothing is cheatable, but the maths is simple: attack vs. defense, plus the 6-element weakness wheel that already exists.

The 6-element wheel

Every Kin has an element — pattern, order, truth, place, wonder, logic — and a readable "tell" (shields on the 3rd beat, heals when you miss). Exploit the weakness for a Break. reuse: elements + gimmicks

This is the one layer of strategy, and it's the same wheel used everywhere else in ArgantaLab, so it's already balanced and tested.

Capture, in a live world

Drop a Kin below a HP threshold and a Befriend prompt appears. Accept it to open the Friendship Window (one question). Succeed and it joins your roster; the Kin isn't "used up," so others nearby can tame their own.

Solves the Pokémon-in-an-MMO problem: taming is a personal moment layered on shared combat.

No harsh death

Lose a fight and you faint — a gentle return to the nearest town, no XP or item loss. Risk is "you wasted time," never "you were punished." Correct for a kids' game and for keeping the tone warm.

Dungeons and the Naga raid ask for a party (2–6, your circle) — the only content that expects teamwork.

09Emblem Wars — Total War, kid-scaled

🧮

Compose

An army = each member's hero (class + grade) plus their befriended Kin, grouped into Regiments by element. Strength = Kin power × bond tier × count.

📍

Deploy

From the War Room, the circle sends Heroes & Regiments to contest a region's shrine. The strategy is composition & targeting — no live micro.

🎬

Resolve

Strength + element bonuses + a small variance roll → winner & margin, played back as a battle report in the Arena. Cosmetic & glory stakes only.

Scope flag: this is auto-resolve, deliberately not a live RTS. A playable tactical battle screen is a much larger build — a Season 2+ decision, not a launch feature.

10Base plan — the MVP ("Season 0")

Goal: prove the core loop is fun and educational. All six worlds explorable from day one, but one dungeon each and the thinnest version of every other system.

ship this first

  • Login via existing ArgantaLab account (no new sign-up)
  • Cove tutorial: move, attack, capture, free starter mount
  • Class select at level 10 — all six available
  • All 6 worlds walkable with Kin hunting grounds
  • Tab-target combat: auto-attack + 4 skills + element wheel
  • Kill-or-tame loop reusing the Kin roster & Friendship Window
  • One dungeon per world, boss = quiz-battle Job Trial
  • Base + Job Level to 99, Grade 1→2→3
  • Rings gate dungeons — the learning bridge, live
  • Home city + mini-castle: Keep, Barracks, Shop, Inn
  • Circle party (2–6) for dungeons, circle-safe chat
  • Existing mount ladder with swim/flier gates

explicitly deferred

  • Emblem Wars, War Room & Arena
  • Full castle: Forge, Archive, facility upgrades
  • Extra dungeons per world & hard modes
  • Gear crafting depth & a farmed gold economy
  • Kin breeding, more skills, sub-path forks
  • PvP duels & player trading
  • Seasonal events & cosmetic drops
  • Bespoke pixel art (starts on placeholder tilesets)

Everything here is a data or content addition onto the MVP's schemas — not a rebuild. That is the whole point of designing the slots now.

11Scale plan — how depth arrives without rewrites

Dungeons as an array

Each region ships one dungeon but the shape is dungeons: DungeonDef[] from day one. Season 2 hard-modes and event dungeons are new rows, not new code.

Castle as a template

Facilities are data-driven. Unlocking the Forge or War Room later is flipping a flag and shipping its panel — the city shell already renders them.

Skills as ranks

Four skills, ranks 1→5. Later seasons add a Tier-2 fork (the RO branch we deferred) without breaking any saved character.

Wars on the element wheel

Emblem Wars re-use the same element maths as combat, so the strategic layer inherits balance for free when it's switched on.

Kin & mounts already scale

The roster, evolution tiers and 10-step mount ladder exist and grow as content — Kingdom of Kin adds zero parallel systems.

Content is shared with ArgantaLab

New learning worlds, drills and Keepers added to the core app appear in the MMO automatically. One content pipeline, two products.

12What's missing & the risks to close

Honest audit. These are the gaps between "great concept" and "shippable game," each with a proposed fix.

1. The learning-in-play loop

A kid could progress without answering a question — fatal for an ed-game.

Fix: the 3-touchpoint bridge (rings-gate + tame-question + quiz-trials). Needs your sign-off.

2. Combat feel

"Tab-target vs. action" changes the whole engine and the fun.

Fix: ship tab-target + cooldowns (simplest, kid-friendly); revisit action-combat only if playtests feel flat.

3. Economy has one currency

Diamonds (learned) buy cosmetics — but there's no farmed currency for gear/potions.

Fix: add gold (dropped by Kin) for consumables & gear; keep diamonds strictly cosmetic. Season 1.

4. Gear depth is undefined

The Forge exists in name only; no gear tiers or stats yet.

Fix: MVP ships fixed starter gear per grade; crafting/upgrades are a Season 1 layer.

5. Retention loop

What brings a kid back tomorrow?

Fix: daily mission board + Kin-care diamond harvest + ArgantaCup competitions — all already exist in ArgantaLab.

6. Pacing & balance

1→99 across six worlds is a lot; level bands are a guess.

Fix: tune to short sessions; treat the bands as first-draft numbers to playtest, not commitments.

7. Real-time capture UX

Turn-based taming inside a live shared world is unusual.

Fix: the personal "Befriend prompt → Friendship Window" pattern; the Kin isn't consumed from the shared spawn.

8. Art & tech pipeline

Bespoke pixel art is coming later; devices vary.

Fix: build on placeholder tilesets behind a swappable art seam (as KinQuest already does); PixiJS + PWA runs on low-end devices.

13Roadmap

Phase 0De-risk

Vertical slice

Two players move & fight in one Colyseus room, server-authoritative. Proves the hardest technical risk (real-time, cheat-proof) before any content.

Phase 1Core loop

One world, end to end

Cove → class → Numeria → hunt/tame → one dungeon → grade-up. The full loop in a single world, learning bridge live. This is the "is it fun?" gate.

Phase 2MVP / Season 0

All six worlds + mini-castles

Roll the proven loop across all six regions, home cities, mounts, party dungeons, circle chat. Soft-launch to ArgantaLab families.

Phase 3Social endgame

Castles & Emblem Wars

Full castle facilities, the War Room, and auto-resolved circle wars. The Suikoden + Total War layers switch on.

Phase 4+Live game

Seasons & depth

Extra dungeons, gear crafting, gold economy, sub-path forks, seasonal events, bespoke art. The game becomes a live service.
