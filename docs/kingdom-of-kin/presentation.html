03The learning bridge — the special sauce

Where does the learning actually happen?

Three touchpoints. Learning is the key, never a tax on every swing.

the gate

Rings unlock dungeons

Your daily ArgantaLab rings are your Focus. Fill a world's ring, its dungeon & Job Trial open. Neglect it, the gate stays shut.

the light touch

A question to tame

To befriend a weakened Kin you answer one quick question — the existing Friendship Window. Capturing is a joyful micro-moment.

the real test

Trials are quiz-battles

Keeper & Job Trials are real quiz-battles at the child's year level. To grade up, you prove the maths.

⚑ Open decision: this model is the proposed answer, not yet signed off — it's the dial we turn for more or less learning pressure.

06Battle system

Kid-simple, tech-light, readable at a glance.

Tab-target, not twitch

Tap a Kin to target, auto-attack begins, fire up to 4 skills on cooldown. No aiming or dodging — friendly for young hands and weak devices. Server-authoritative so nothing's cheatable.

The 6-element wheel

Every Kin has an element — pattern, order, truth, place, wonder, logic — and a readable tell (shields on the 3rd beat). Exploit the weakness for a Break. reuse

Capture, live

Drop a Kin low → a Befriend prompt → the Friendship Window (one question). It's not consumed from the shared spawn, so others can tame their own.

No harsh death

Lose and you faint — gentle return to town, no XP or item loss. Risk is wasted time, never punishment. Dungeons & the Naga raid want a party of 2–6.

  • All 6 worlds walkable, one dungeon each

  • Bespoke pixel art (starts on placeholders)

All of it is data onto MVP schemas — not a rebuild. That's why we design the slots now.

10Honest audit — what's missing

The gaps between great concept and shippable game.

1 · Learning-in-play loop

A kid could progress without a single question — fatal.

Fix: the 3-touchpoint bridge. Needs your sign-off.

2 · Combat feel

Tab-target vs. action changes the whole engine.

Fix: ship tab-target; revisit only if playtests feel flat.

3 · One currency

Diamonds are cosmetic — nothing farmed for gear/potions.

Fix: add gold from Kin drops; keep diamonds cosmetic.

4 · Gear depth

The Forge is a name only.

Fix: fixed gear per grade at MVP; crafting in S1.

5 · Retention

What brings a kid back tomorrow?

Fix: daily board + Kin-care harvest + ArgantaCup — all exist.

6 · Pacing & balance

1→99 across six worlds; bands are guesses.

Fix: short sessions; treat bands as first-draft numbers.
