# The Reactor

The unifying motif. A **Tony-Stark arc reactor with 7 layers**, reframed for *energy & the digital subsurface* — SLB-Lumi-adjacent, but Arganta's own. Back to [[00-Home]]. Styled by [[Design-Language]]. Aligns with the built reactor in `apps/hq/src/reactor` (memory [[jarvis-reactor-ws2]] — component-based 7-layer model).

## What it means
The reactor is **Arganta Core made visible** — the single engine that powers three companies. Gold core (constant), outer rings tint to whichever company is speaking.

## The 7 layers (core + 6 rings)
| # | Layer | Meaning | Energy reading (strata) |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | **Core** | Arganta Core — the intelligence / the operator | The reservoir source |
| L1 | Data | Truth, ground-truth, the real record | Subsurface data |
| L2 | Models | Reasoning, geomodels, ML | Static/earth model |
| L3 | Agents | Automation, workflows | Simulation / workflow |
| L4 | Interfaces | Products & apps | Decision surface |
| L5 | Circle | People, family, community | The operators |
| L6 | World | Deployment, scale, the market | Field / production |

For **ArgantaEnergy** the rings literally read as **geological strata** — the reactor becomes a glowing subsurface column. This is the SLB-Lumi feeling: engineered intelligence lighting up the invisible underground.

## Three states — the "build once" assets
1. **Intro splash** — black → single spark → core ignites → 7 layers bloom axially → hold. (~3–5s) Opens every film.
2. **Ambient loop** — slow rotation, energy particles, breathing glow. Website hero + app loading + reactor page.
3. **Ending / outro** — layers settle inward → collapse to the Arganta mark. (~3–4s) Closes every film.

## How it's produced (asset ↔ code)
- 🟣 **Higgsfield** (Track E in the punchlist): reactor **core 3D mesh** (`image_to_3d`), **cinematic ignition/explosion films** (`kling3_0`), **7-layer emissive/material renders** (`nano_banana`), **turntable loop** (`kling3_0_turbo`).
- ⚙️ **Code** (me): the *live interactive* reactor + Reactor Builder tab in `apps/hq/src/reactor` — consumes the mesh + textures; procedural energy, reactive to scroll/voice.
- The splash + outro films are rendered once and dropped into all four [[Cinematic-Launch]] films.

## Energy-specific variant
Deep `energy-blue → teal` outer rings, subsurface strata rings, seismic shimmer, a drill-to-core camera move. This variant is the visual spine of [[Film-ArgantaEnergy]] and the [[Brand-Architecture|ArgantaEnergy]] platform (GeaVision) hero.
