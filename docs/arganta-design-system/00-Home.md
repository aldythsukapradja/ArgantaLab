## The three non-negotiables
1. **One system** — no film, page, or app invents its own palette/motion. They inherit from [[Design-Language]].
2. **Reactor everywhere** — the same reactor opens and closes every film and anchors every hero surface. Built once ([[The-Reactor]]), reused literally.
3. **Real & factual** — only real product UIs, real founder credibility, real data. No fabricated metrics presented as live. See [[Cinematic-Launch#The real & factual rule]].
