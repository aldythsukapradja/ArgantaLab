# Brand Architecture

Arganta.ai as an **endorsed house** — one master intelligence brand, three companies that barely overlap. Back to [[00-Home]]. Colors from [[Design-Language]].

## Arganta.ai (master)
The holding intelligence company. Signature = gold [[The-Reactor]] core. Positioning: *one operator, powered by a reactor of intelligence, running three worlds.* Credibility is real (founder: 15 yrs digital subsurface, 20+ publications — memory [[arganta-persona-canon]]).

## The 3×3 portfolio

| Company | Three offerings | Positioning | Light |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ArgantaLife** | Connect · Grow · Play | Consumer & family ecosystem | Coral `#FF7A59` |
| **ArgantaEnergy** | Consulting · Academy · Platform | Energy AI & digital subsurface business | Energy blue `#2E7CF6` |
| **ArgantaStudio** | Concept · Create · Transform | AI creative & digital-transformation studio | Violet `#A06CE8` |

### ArgantaLife — families & everyday consumers
- **Connect →** KinetikCircle (Family OS: calendar, memories, AI assistant)
- **Grow →** ArgantaLab (kids learning, AI education, growth analytics)
- **Play →** LashiraBloom (cozy family MMO & gamified experiences)

### ArgantaEnergy — oil & gas + industrial AI
- **Consulting →** digital transformation, reservoir & subsurface consulting, AI-workflow implementation
- **Academy →** professional training, AI for geoscience, Petrel/GeaVision workshops, corporate upskilling
- **Platform →** GeaVision (lightweight exploration & static-modeling platform), future agentic subsurface workflows, decision-support apps

### ArgantaStudio — helping others build AI products
- **Concept →** product strategy, UX/UI, AI architecture, MVP planning
- **Create →** web apps, AI agents, games, websites, marketing assets, videos
- **Transform →** AI automation, company digitalization, internal operating systems, agentic workflows, deployment & scaling

## Why this holds
- **ArgantaLife** sells consumer products.
- **ArgantaEnergy** sells industry expertise + software.
- **ArgantaStudio** sells creation services + AI transformation.
