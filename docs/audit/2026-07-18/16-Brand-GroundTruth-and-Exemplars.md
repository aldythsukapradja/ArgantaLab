## The two conflicts, resolved

**C1 — Two metaphors: subsurface (doctrine) vs whiteboard (audit [[10-Brand-UIUX-Battle-Test]]).**
Resolution: **they are different layers of the brand, keep both.** The *subsurface/cross-section* is the **founder brand** — Aldyth's authentic territory (15 years modeling hidden worlds → making invisible growth visible), lives in build-in-public, decks, LinkedIn, the About story. The *whiteboard* is the **product brand** — the customer's own artifact, lives in the launch film, onboarding, product marketing. They even share a thesis — *making the invisible visible* — which becomes the bridging line: "I spent fifteen years making the underground visible. Then I noticed the most invisible system in my life was on our kitchen whiteboard."

**C2 — The desire map is pre-consolidation (5 products, 8 audiences).**
The doctrine's mechanism survives; the map's rows must collapse per [[01-Vision-Critique]]. Wedge-era map = 4 rows: Organizing parent (word: **PROOF** → extend to *RELIEF+PROOF*), Co-parent (**EASE** — the doctrine's "participation, not tracking" row is excellent and Ollie-differentiating), Kids (**MINE/REAL** — held for Kids Workspace phase), Investors (**LEGIBILITY** — feeds [[12-Dossier-Gaps-and-Fundraising-Roadmap]]). Founders/Experts rows = founder-brand channel only. Update `doctrineData.ts` desire map when the freeze executes.

**Product-brand lane (Arganta):**
- **Skylight (family calendar HW)** — the category's proven paid/organic playbook: real-family UGC demos, "day in the life with the family display," spouse-tag mechanics. Copy the *format*, beat them on intelligence (they show a screen; you show an assistant).
