## 1 · Monetization strategy (consolidated, finally in one place)

**Model: freemium family subscription — one price, whole household.** Anchored to repo's monetization plan ($4.99/mo · $39.99/yr) but now with the *strategy* around it:

- **Free tier = the Today board + calendar + basic assistant** (limited memory window, no proactive digests). Free must be genuinely useful — the free board IS the acquisition and referral surface ([[18-Remaining-Success-Gaps]] S2); a crippled free tier kills the family→family loop.
- **Paid ($4.99/mo family) = the memory + intelligence layer:** unlimited family memory, Sunday-reset digests, proactive agents (year 1's three agents, [[19-Meta-Audit]] M5), kids' learning workspace + progress reveals, fridge mode (S6). **Principle: charge for the moat, give away the utility** — the inverse of Cozi (who paywalled utility and stalled). Maps to [[17-Competitor-Benchmark]]: the 20-weight axes are the paid axes.
- **Pricing psychology:** one family price (never per-seat — per-seat punishes the exact behavior you want, adding members); annual push at onboarding day ~30 after the first Sunday-reset habit forms; regional pricing (ID price point likely Rp 39–49k/mo — validate in interviews; Qatar/GCC can carry $6.99–9.99).
- **Never:** ads, data monetization, in-app currency for the family app (diamonds stay inside the kids' game layer, cosmetic-only per the two-currency rule), dark-pattern trials. Each "never" is a trust-page line ([[09-Cybersecurity-Battle-Test]]).
- **Year-2+ expansion revenue (design now, ship later):** B2B2C school/community licenses (P3 machinery as revenue), premium sovereign tier ("your family's AI never leaves your country" — GCC data-sovereignty angle is real money), hardware-lite partnership (fridge-mode tablet bundle), grandparent/caregiver seats as free virality not revenue.
- **Unit-economics guardrail** (feeds M3 model): inference cost per paying family must stay <10% of ARPU; the tiered router ([[07-Technology-Blueprint-2yr]] R1) is what makes $4.99 gross-margin-viable — route free-tier queries to cost-class 0/1 only.
