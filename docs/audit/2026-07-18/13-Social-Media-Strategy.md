# Social Media Strategy

Solves [[03-Gap-Analysis]] G1 (distribution) with the assets already live (Post Studio + Buffer→IG pipeline). Governing rule: **one brand (Arganta), one audience (the overloaded parent), one story (the whiteboard), every platform.**

## The face question first: AI influencer vs. real face vs. faceless

**Verdict: real founder face, product-assisted. The AI influencer is dead for this company — permanently, not paused.**

Reasoning, cold: 
- **Trust is the product.** You are asking parents to hand an AI their children's data. An AI-generated spokesperson selling that is not just off-brand — it is *disconfirming evidence* of your core promise. One "wait, is she real?" comment poisons the well.
- **Your differentiator is Customer Zero authenticity** ([[04-Emotional-Brand-Audit]]). An avatar cannot own the whiteboard story. Faceless can't either — faceless works for tools (productivity/ASMR niches), not for "trust me with your family."
- **The persona-canon decision was already right** — founder as real digital twin, launch alone. Make it permanent.
- **Compromise that keeps the pipeline useful:** *founder face on camera; AI does everything around the face* — scripts, edits, b-roll, thumbnails, carousels via your existing fabric. And **kids never on camera identifiable** (backs of heads, hands, voices at most — this is a brand safety rule AND a marketing asset: "we don't post our kids' faces; that's the company you're trusting").
- Comfort hack if camera-shy: voice-over + hands + screen + whiteboard shots outperform talking heads in this niche anyway.

## Platform-by-platform

### TikTok + IG Reels (one production, two channels) — **the engine, 60% of effort**
- **Job:** reach strangers; short-form is the only zero-follower discovery machine.
- **Content pillars (weekly rhythm, 4–5 posts/wk):**
  1. **The Whiteboard Chronicles** — the flagship serial. Ep.1: "My wife ran our family from this whiteboard for 6 years." Real board, real handwriting. Serialized = follows.
  2. **Mental-load truths** — 15–30s relatable observations ("the default parent knows the shoe sizes"), text-on-screen. Shareable-to-spouse is the KPI; the send-to-partner DM is your growth loop.
  3. **Build-in-public, parent edition** — "I'm a dad building the assistant my family needed"; demo the magic moments ([[04-Emotional-Brand-Audit]]): whiteboard-photo import, "ask Argi," Sunday reset.
  4. **Kid-learning reveals** (consented, faceless) — "my daughter thinks she's playing; here's her fractions curve."
- **Bilingual advantage:** produce ID + EN versions of winners (AI dubbing/subs via your media stack). Indonesian family-TikTok is enormous and under-served by exactly this product ([[05-Unicorn-Path]] SEA beachhead).

### Instagram (feed/carousels) — **the shop window, 20%**
- Job: convert profile visits from Reels into waitlist. Carousels = Post Studio's home turf: mental-load frameworks, "5 things the default parent tracks," launch-story slides. Bio → waitlist. Grid aesthetic per [[10-Brand-UIUX-Battle-Test]]: warm, whiteboard-motif, zero gradient-tech.

### YouTube — **the trust vault, 15%**
- Job: depth for people already interested; search shelf-life ("family organization app," "mental load"). 
- Cadence: 1–2/mo long-form only — the 90s launch film (pinned everywhere), "How I built an AI for my family" builder-story series (taps the huge build-in-public audience — your *second* honest audience), quarterly deep demos. Shorts = repost winners from TikTok, free.

### LinkedIn — **the fundraising channel, 5%, deliberately misused**
- Job: NOT user acquisition — angels, edtech operators, future hires, press. Weekly single post: milestone updates, honest metrics ("week 6: 41% of pilot families still do the Sunday reset"), founder lessons. This *is* the investor-update pipeline of [[12-Dossier-Gaps-and-Fundraising-Roadmap]] Track B, in public.

### Explicitly not now: X (no parent density for you), Facebook (revisit at scale — parent groups matter later), Pinterest (sleeper for "family organization" search; carousels repost there in 10 min/wk if free).

## The machine (repo-correlated)
Post Studio + Buffer = IG/LinkedIn scheduling (live today). Video Builder + media stack = subtitles, dubs, thumbnails. **Rule from [[02-Repo-Reality-Audit]]: the machine gets zero new features until it has published 30 posts.** Batch production: one 2-hr filming session/wk → 5 clips; founder time ≤1 day/wk total ([[12-Dossier-Gaps-and-Fundraising-Roadmap]] Track C).

## Metrics (only these)
Waitlist signups/wk (north star) · send-to-partner shares on pillar-2 · watch-through on Whiteboard Chronicles · follower count explicitly NOT a KPI.

## 90-day social plan
Weeks 1–2: handles secured, bios unified, waitlist page live, film Ep.1. Weeks 3–8: full rhythm, kill/double pillars by data at wk 4. Weeks 9–12: launch film premiere across all four, pilot-family testimonials (voices, not faces), LinkedIn milestone thread.
