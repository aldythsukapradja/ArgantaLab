# Competitor Benchmark

Method first, scores second, so the numbers are challengeable and repeatable.

## Method
1. **Frame (Dunford):** competitive alternatives are *what the buyer would do instead* — including the whiteboard + WhatsApp group + one parent's memory ("Status Quo" — the true market leader).
2. **Criteria & weights** derived from the wedge JTBD ([[16-Brand-GroundTruth-and-Exemplars]] desire map): Assistant intelligence 20 · Family memory/context 20 (the moat axes get the weight) · Logistics core (calendar/lists) 15 · Kids' growth loop 15 · Multi-user family UX 10 · Trust/privacy posture 10 · Price/accessibility 5 · Distribution muscle 5. Scores 0–5 per criterion; weighted total /100.
3. **Evidence class** (doctrine discipline): these scores are **class-C desk research from training knowledge (cutoff Jan 2026)** — directionally reliable, individually verify before publishing any comparison externally. Refresh via store listings + trials during pilot weeks 3–4 ([[12-Dossier-Gaps-and-Fundraising-Roadmap]] D4 closes with this).

## What the matrix says (and doesn't)
1. **Nobody scores above ~46.** The category has no dominant product — validating both timing and the wedge ([[14-Pivot-and-Holding-Strategy]]). The real opponent is Status Quo at 44 — free, installed everywhere, zero onboarding. Marketing must beat *it*, not Cozi.
2. **Arganta's paper score (~69) is entirely conditional** on the two 20-weight moat axes actually shipping (memory store, then kids loop). Ship logistics-only and you're a 40-something — indistinguishable. Quantitative confirmation that [[07-Technology-Blueprint-2yr]] R3 is the company.
3. **Ollie is the only AI-native threat**; its weakness rows are memory, kids, and trust — attack there, never on logistics polish where incumbents win.
4. **Skylight/Hearth prove families pay** ($300+ hardware!) for a *glanceable shared surface* — strong evidence for the Today-board pivot ([[10-Brand-UIUX-Battle-Test]] U1) and for a future "old tablet on the fridge" mode (cheap, differentiating; added to [[18-Remaining-Success-Gaps]]).
5. **Google/Apple** score on distribution, not product coherence — treat as channels ([[08-Blueprint-Battle-Test]] Attack 2 holds).
6. **SEA lens:** every named competitor is US/EU-first, none bilingual-ID, none WhatsApp-native in behavior. The regional matrix is effectively **Status Quo vs Arganta** — the beachhead argument ([[05-Unicorn-Path]] Stage 3) survives the benchmark.

Re-run this matrix 30 min/quarter from store listings + changelogs; alert-level events: Ollie ships memory/kids features · Skylight ships an assistant · a bilingual SEA family app appears. Log deltas in this folder.
