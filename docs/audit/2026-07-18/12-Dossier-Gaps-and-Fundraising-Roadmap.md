## 2 · Full roadmap — building on [[06-Wayforward-90-Days]], now with the fundraising track interleaved

### Track A (product) — unchanged: Days 1–7 debt · 8–30 wedge assembly (per [[10-Brand-UIUX-Battle-Test]]: Today-board-first) · 31–60 pilot+interviews · 61–90 public wedge, 100 families.

### Track B (dossier/fundraise) — runs alongside, ~1 day/week cap:
- **Weeks 1–2:** D1 incorporation decision + start · D9 KPI definitions · D2 cap-table page.
- **Weeks 3–4:** D4 competitive teardown · D5 regulatory memo · D7 founder page.
- **Weeks 5–8:** D3 financial model (fed by real pilot infra costs) · D8 data room assembly · begin *relationship-building* — 10 warm intros/coffee chats with angels & family-tech operators, **explicitly not raising** ("building in public, raising after pilot data").
- **Weeks 9–12:** D6 evidence pack from pilot · D10 deck v1 · launch film doubles as pitch opener.
- **Month 4–5 — Angel/pre-seed raise:** $300–600k SAFE at $3–6M cap ([[11-Valuation-Report]] row 2). Target: angels who are parents, edtech operators, SEA consumer funds' scout programs. Use of funds: 12-mo runway, first growth hire, paid-inference budget, pen test.
- **Months 6–12 — Stage-1 execution:** 1,000 paying families ([[05-Unicorn-Path]]), Phase-2 tech (memory intelligence, voice, push-native), monthly investor update email (starts month 4 — the update *is* the seed pipeline).
- **Months 12–15 — Seed:** $1.5–2.5M at $8–15M pre on the retention curve + revenue. Narrative: "the family memory moat is turning on" (Phase 2 shipped, reveal-card loop measurable).
- **Months 15–24:** Phase 3–4 of [[07-Technology-Blueprint-2yr]] (native, integrations, knowledge graph, Kids Workspace GA) → Series-A posture: $1M+ ARR trajectory, the un-copyable memory graph as the story.

### Track C (standing constraints)
Freeze list holds ([[06-Wayforward-90-Days]]) · security phases ship with their product phases ([[09-Cybersecurity-Battle-Test]]) · Phase-4 tech gated at 300 active families ([[08-Blueprint-Battle-Test]]) · founder time split ~4 days product / 1 day dossier+distribution — inverted to 2/3 during raise months only.
