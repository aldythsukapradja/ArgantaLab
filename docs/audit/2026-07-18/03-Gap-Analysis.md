# Gap Analysis

Ranked by how lethal each gap is. Context: [[02-Repo-Reality-Audit]]. Remedies: [[06-Wayforward-90-Days]].

## G1 — Distribution (lethal)
Zero channel to strangers. No waitlist, no community, no SEO, no app-store presence, no viral loop. The Buffer→IG pipeline is live but has been aimed at brand-building for five sub-brands rather than at acquiring users for one product. **A great product with no distribution is a diary.** This gap alone caps the company at N=1 family forever.

## G2 — Demand evidence (lethal)
N=1 (your family) is a beautiful story and a terrible sample. No interviews, no fake-door test, no pricing signal. The $4.99 monetization plan, the diamond economy, the gift vault — all designed against imagined users. Risk: months of economy-tuning for an economy nobody is in.

## G3 — Focus / WIP limit (lethal, self-inflicted)
Four companies in one repo ([[01-Vision-Critique]]). ~40 plan docs, many marked NOT built. The 95%-done migration pathology. The fix is not motivation — it is a *structural WIP limit*: one product, one launch, definition-of-done = "live for strangers," enforced in [[06-Wayforward-90-Days]].

## G4 — The wedge product itself doesn't exist yet (serious)
Ironically, after all this building, the four wedge items are the least-finished path: family chat assistant on landing is a plan (F1–F5/O1–O8, NOT built); Kinetik has calendar + moments but runs on baked snapshots/guest embeds in places; the assistant brain (`arganta-chat-brain`) is new and unwired to family data. The wedge requires *assembly of existing parts*, not new invention — that's the good news.

## G5 — Trust & safety floor for a kids/family AI product (serious)
An AI assistant with family memory, used by children, needs before any public launch: parental consent flow, data deletion/export that actually works, no-training guarantee in writing, prompt-injection hardening on anything the assistant can act on, COPPA/GDPR-K posture (Indonesia + wherever you launch). "Privacy by product design" is currently a sentence, not a feature list. This is also a *marketing asset* if done visibly.

## G6 — Reliability & ops maturity (serious)
No CI/tests on customer apps, known-broken edge fn (llm-proxy), pending migrations, OneDrive-hosted git repo (slow, sync-risk to your working tree). Fine for a lab; not for other people's family calendars. A family product that loses a calendar event once is deleted.

## G7 — Solo-founder concentration (structural)
One person is CEO, CTO, designer, artist, marketer, and the QA department, with AI quota as the effective engineering budget (today: 3%). Consequences: bus factor 1, and build-capacity volatility. Mitigations: ruthless scope (G3), and eventually a co-founder or first hire in the one skill you don't enjoy — distribution.

## G8 — Business model untested (moderate)
Family subscription is plausible (Cozi, Ollie precedents) but unvalidated. The two-currency game economy adds economy-design risk before there's an economy. Defer everything except one simple sub price test.

## G9 — Brand fragmentation (moderate)
Arganta / ArgantaLab / KinetikCircle / Kinetik / KinQuest / LashiraBloom / Kingdom of Kin / Arganta Core / five influencer personas. A stranger cannot hold this. The wedge strategy already fixes it: **one name (Arganta), workspaces inside**. Kids' game keeps its flavor *inside* the Kids Workspace.

## What is NOT a gap (stop worrying about these)
- Technical capability, AI infrastructure, design skill — all surplus.
- More studios/tooling — you are over-tooled, not under-tooled.
- Competitor feature parity — Ollie's features matter less than your story + moat ([[05-Unicorn-Path]]).
