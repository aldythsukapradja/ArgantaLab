# Meta-Audit — Gaps in Notes 00–18

The dossier audited everything except itself. Honest pass, angel-investor skepticism applied to my own work. These are inputs for the Opus expansion ([[20-Opus-Handoff-1yr-Blueprint]]).

## M1 — Evidence quality: the audit is desk-bound
Almost every external claim (Ollie's funding/features, Skylight/Hearth playbooks, SEA pre-seed norms, retention benchmarks like "≥40% wk-4") is **training-knowledge class-C, uncited, unverified as of today**. The audit practices worse claims discipline than the founder's own F9 doctrine demands. → Opus task: verify with live web research; attach sources; adjust any number that moves.

## M2 — Echo-chamber convergence
All 19 notes were written by one analyst (me) in one sitting, and they converge suspiciously neatly ("everything says run the 90 days"). Real advisory work has dissent. Missing: a genuine **red-team note arguing the opposite thesis** — e.g. "the wedge is wrong; kids-learning-first (P2) is the better lead because the engines are the only finished asset and parents demonstrably pay for learning, not logistics." That case was scored down in [[14-Pivot-and-Holding-Strategy]] but never argued at full strength. → Opus: write the strongest possible P2-first and B2B2C-first cases before re-affirming (or overturning) the wedge.

## M3 — The financial model gap is load-bearing, not clerical
[[11-Valuation-Report]] and [[12-Dossier-Gaps-and-Fundraising-Roadmap]] D3 wave at unit economics ($0.10–0.30 inference/family, $4.99 ARPU, CAC "assumption") without a single built spreadsheet. The $1B math in [[05-Unicorn-Path]] divides two big numbers. Missing: cohort model, CAC-by-channel estimate, inference-cost model *per signature moment*, break-even families count, runway math under the real quota/budget constraint (S12). → Opus: build the actual model, three scenarios.

## M4 — The 90-day plan's capacity math was never checked
Solo founder + day job? (audit never even established whether founding is full-time — a material omission), family obligations, 3% AI quota events, 4d product + 1d dossier + social batch day = **more than 5 days**. The plan risks being aspirational the way the repo's own plans were. → Opus: rebuild the calendar bottom-up in founder-hours/week with an explicit capacity number from the founder, and cut scope to fit — the audit repeated the founder's own overcommitment pattern it diagnosed (ironic; note it).

## M5 — The Agentic Layer is under-analyzed
The founder's stack includes a real agent substrate (Agent OS v2 design, Bridge approval gates, C-suite offices, mission runner, MCP servers). The audit froze it ([[06-Wayforward-90-Days]]) and reused only the approval-gate pattern ([[07-Technology-Blueprint-2yr]] Phase 4). Unexamined: **agents as the product's core differentiator in the 2027 market** — proactive family agents (renewal watcher, school-email triage, gift planner) may be the actual "assistant" families expect by next year, not Q&A chat. The blueprint treats agency as a Phase-4 garnish; competitors (and the Ollie/OpenAI trajectory) may make it table stakes sooner. → Opus: dedicate a full workstream to the family agentic layer — safety model, approval UX, which 3 agents ship in year 1.

## M6 — Indonesia is a slogan, not an analysis
"SEA beachhead" recurs in 6 notes with zero specifics: no market sizing (ID family-app spend, payment rails — GoPay/OVO vs cards, family-plan norms), no ID competitor scan, no cultural analysis (extended-family/multi-generation households change the *product model*, not just language — "circles" may fit ID family structure better than Western nuclear-family apps, a possible structural advantage the audit never explored), no launch-market decision (ID-first vs global-English-first is THE go-to-market fork and it's undecided). → Opus: full ID market workstream; make the launch-geography call explicitly.

## M7 — Distribution strategy assumes organic works
[[13-Social-Media-Strategy]] is 100% organic short-form. No paid-acquisition thinking (family apps historically scale on paid social + UGC ads — Skylight's actual engine), no channel CAC hypotheses, no fallback if the founder's content simply doesn't hit (base rate: most don't). → Opus: paid-channel plan B with budget thresholds, plus school/community partnership channel (P3's machinery as a *channel*, not a pivot).

## M8 — Emotional layer lacks a measurement plan
[[04-Emotional-Brand-Audit]] defines 5 signature moments; nothing defines how *felt emotion* is measured beyond retention proxies (PMF surveys, Sean Ellis "very disappointed" %, NPS timing, qualitative ritual interviews). "A lot of users like it emotionally" — the founder's stated goal — has no instrument. → Opus: emotional-metrics framework.

## M9 — Organizational design is a paragraph
"Hire #1 = growth, month 6" is the entire org plan. A 1-year 1B-track blueprint needs: hiring sequence with triggers, comp/equity bands, what stays founder-only, what the AI-agent workforce covers vs humans (this company's org chart is uniquely half-AI — genuinely novel, deserves real design), advisory board targets. → Opus: org workstream.

## M10 — Smaller holes, listed for completeness
"1-year to $1B" framing must be corrected honestly (1-year plan *on a 1B trajectory*; no consumer company hits $1B valuation in year 1 — promising otherwise would be audit malpractice) · churn/void scenarios for the memory moat (what if families *don't* feed memory? cold moat = no moat) · App Store 30% tax vs web pricing unmodeled · data-migration/lock-in ethics as brand risk · grandparent/caregiver persona absent from desire map · no timeline for killing the fake-door test if signups are strong but activation is weak.

## What survives the meta-audit unchanged
The freeze list · the wedge-passes-the-Google-test analysis (M2 must attack it, but it starts strong) · the security architecture verdict · valuation *ranges* (method-triangulated, conservative) · S13 canon-sync · the core instruction (evidence over analysis). The meta-audit sharpens the dossier; it does not overturn it.
