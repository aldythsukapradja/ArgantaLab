# Arganta Full Audit — Executive Summary

> **One-line verdict:** You have built a *studio* that can build anything, and in doing so you have not yet built a *product* that anyone outside your family uses. The branding wedge you brainstormed ([[01-Vision-Critique]]) is correct — and it implicitly indicts ~80% of the repo.

## The three headline findings

**1. Inversion of effort.** The repo's center of gravity is `apps/hq` — a founder-facing command center with ~20 "studios" (Video, Music, Post, Character Forge, Skill Forge, Biography, AI Influencer, Reactor, Cognitive Cortex, Vault graph, Command Center…). The *customer-facing* product (Kinetik family app + landing chat) is a fraction of the codebase. A unicorn is priced on users, not on internal tooling. See [[02-Repo-Reality-Audit]].

**2. Shipping to repo ≠ shipping to users.** The recurring pattern across ~40 plan docs and memory: "BUILT+verified… migration PENDING… worker redeploy PENDING… NOT built." Many features are one un-run SQL file away from working, indefinitely. There is no evidence anywhere in the repo of a single external user, a waitlist, an interview note, or a retention number. See [[03-Gap-Analysis]].

**3. The emotional asset is real and unused.** The Customer Zero story — the wife's whiteboard, the founder asking "what's today's schedule," kids actually learning 30–45 min/day — is a genuinely differentiated, Pixar-grade founding story. It appears in a strategy doc; it appears nowhere in the product or the landing page. See [[04-Emotional-Brand-Audit]].

## Verdict on the wedge strategy
The ChatGPT brainstorm ("AI Family Assistant, connected workspaces, launch only Family Workspace + Assistant + Calendar + Moments") is **directionally right** and better than anything in the repo's own planning docs, because it is the first document that says *no* to things. Its weaknesses: no distribution plan, no pricing test, no named ICP beyond "families," and it still smuggles in a "Founder Workspace (future)". Critique in [[01-Vision-Critique]].
