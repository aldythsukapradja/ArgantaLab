## 3. Extracted Principle
For every premium creative-AI tool there is now a $0 alternative that is "good enough" for
draft/volume work. The moat is moving from *model access* → *taste + workflow*. Budget premium
tools only where quality is brand-critical; default to free for drafts and experiments.
