## Week gates
- **W1–2** — planner habit: ≥4 planner days/week
- **W3–4** — learning habit: kid streak ≥5 days via [[argantalab|ArgantaLabs]]
- **W5–6** — bloom habit: family plot watered in [[lashirabloom|LashiraBloom]]
- **W7–8** — loop proof: all three surfaces touched in one day, twice a week
