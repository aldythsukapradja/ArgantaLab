# LashiraBloom

> [!note] 🟢 CANONICAL v1 (status: draft) · LashiraBloom · updated 2026-07-03

A Stardew-inspired family MMORPG, inspired by the founder's daughter. The farm is the *retention layer* that makes leaving the ecosystem feel like abandoning a garden. Rationale in [[decision-lashirabloom-as-retention-world|Decision — LashiraBloom as Retention World]].

## The unification bet
LashiraBloom absorbs two loose ends:
- the **Arena** progress from [[argantalab|ArgantaLabs]] (kids' effort becomes world power)
- the **minifarm** from [[kinetikcircle|KinetikCircle]] (household rituals water real crops)

Adults play, kids learn: grown-ups can farm freely, but growth multipliers come from the kids' learning streaks — the household literally blooms when the children do. Currency flows through the [[argons-economy|Argons Economy]].

## World pillars
- A shared plot per family circle, visible to every member
- Seasons synchronized with [[argantalab|ArgantaLabs]] rank seasons
- No dark-pattern timers: crops wait politely, they never rot

Playable farm slice built; economy hookup and family plot sync are next — see [[product-roadmap|Product Roadmap]] and the pilot gates in [[family-pilot-plan|Family Pilot Plan]].
