# Brief — OG / link-card image (ArgantaLab)

**Read `../BRAND.md` first.** Style anchor: `../refs/horizontal-dark.png`.

- **Size:** 1200×630 (OpenGraph / Twitter summary_large_image / LinkedIn share)
- **Where it shows:** every time anyone shares `lab.arganta.app` anywhere. This is the
  brand's most-seen image outside the feed — it must read at thumbnail size.
- **Text:** the wordmark + tagline ARE composited here (unlike ad heroes) via the
  `ogTemplate`, on the left third. Leave that area quiet.

## Prompt

> Wide cinematic composition on a night-ink `#070A12` ground. In the right third,
> a luminous wireframe cube with a glowing white core, rim-lit in cyan-to-purple
> (`#34E5FF` → `#8B5CF6`), a thin orbit ring around it with a single satellite dot.
> A soft radial glow blooms behind it. The left two-thirds are near-empty dark
> space with only a faint receding grid — deliberately quiet, reserved for a
> wordmark. Calm, still, premium. Single-source lighting, deep falloff. No text.

## Negative prompt

> text, wordmark, watermark, people, faces, busy left side, stock photo, cartoon,
> neon clutter, lens flare

## Accept / reject

- ✅ Subject right-of-centre, left two-thirds quiet enough to hold the wordmark
- ✅ Still legible as a 300px-wide thumbnail — one shape, high contrast
- ❌ Detail so fine it turns to mush at thumbnail size
