## Prompt

> Vertical composition. A luminous wireframe cube with a bright white core hangs
> in the upper-middle of a vast night-ink `#070A12` void, rim-lit in {{PILLAR_ACCENT}}
> with the brand's cyan-to-pink gradient glancing off its edges. A thin orbit ring
> circles it, one small satellite dot catching the light. Below and around: empty,
> calm darkness with a faint grid receding far into the distance. Cinematic,
> single-source, deep falloff. Vertical negative space in the lower third so copy
> can sit there later. No text, no people.

## Negative prompt

> text, watermark, people, faces, cartoon, clip art, cluttered background,
> neon cyberpunk, lens flare, chrome, stock photo, busy foreground in the lower third

## Accept / reject

- ✅ Focal point in the middle band; lower third quiet enough for a text plate
- ✅ {{PILLAR_ACCENT}} clearly leads
