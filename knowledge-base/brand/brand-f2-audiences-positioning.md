## Positioning statements

**Arganta (master):** For families who want screen time to build something, Arganta is the connected family universe where play, learning, planning and creation share one world — unlike single-purpose apps, everything a child earns and makes feeds the same account, economy and story. RTB: shipped products, open build log, cosmetic-only economy.

**ArgantaLab:** For kids who want to make real things (and parents who want to trust the process), ArgantaLab is the creation studio where playing teaches, building ships, and progress is visible — unlike Duolingo/Khan (consume-only) or Roblox (unbounded), it closes the loop: **Play → Learn → Build → Ship.**

**Kinetik Circle:** For the parent holding the household together, Kinetik Circle turns plans, people and moments into one calm rhythm — unlike Life360's surveillance posture, it's built on *participation, not tracking*.

**LashiraBloom:** For families who want a world of their own, LashiraBloom is the cozy farm the whole family returns to — unlike solo cozy games, growth is shared: adults play, kids learn, the same world blooms.

**Circle HQ (internal):** the founder OS that turns a five-product company into one legible cockpit. Never publicly marketed; its story is told through the build log, not a landing page.
