# F6 — 90-day launch & content plan

Absorbs handoff §§16–17. Home field: `spine.rhythm` + `spine.playbooks`. **One wedge: ArgantaLab.** Everything else earns its launch. Solo-founder cadence throughout; the existing 30-day IG plan folds into Month 1.

## The three months

| Month | Theme | Focus | Gate to next month |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Lab opens** | @argantalab daily-ish (the existing 30-day plan, re-briefed through the new voice); profile pack live; Buffer pipeline proving itself | 20 posts shipped · pipeline friction < 30 min/day · 3 posts that clearly outperformed |
| 2 | **The universe reveals** | www.arganta.app ships (F1 copy); @arganta opens; "One island, five worlds" ecosystem campaign; family beta recruitment for Kinetik Circle begins *through* Lab+Arganta | www live · ≥5 beta families onboarded · Arganta account has 8-week bank |
| 3 | **Build with Arganta** | founder-journey pages live (Spark/Seed decided); first Founder Signal series; LashiraBloom teaser only if playable build exists (else slips — no vapor) | 1 real founder submission OR consciously park the funnel |

## Weekly skeleton (spine.rhythm seed)

| Day | Slot | Series |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | build log | Build of the Week / Founder Signal |
| Wed | product story | pillar rotation (Play/Learn/Build/Ship · The Week Together · Farm Diary) |
| Fri | ship/win | What Kids Learned · real artifact rule |
| Sun *(optional)* | story/poll | native IG interaction, not pipeline |

## Campaign playbooks (spine.playbooks seeds)

**launch-lab** (M1): 30-day plan → hybrid MCP drafts → founder approve → Buffer queue. Metric: posts shipped, saves, profile taps. Stop rule: engagement < 1% for 2 weeks → change hooks, not cadence.
**reveal-universe** (M2): 5-post arc — island map → one post per world → "one account" closer. Assets: ChatGPT P1 OG/story sets.
**family-beta** (M2–3): recruit via Lab audience; ask = 15-min weekly feedback; give = founder access + named thanks. **No paid ads until organic proves a hook** (`working-hypothesis`).
**build-with-arganta** (M3): founder story → journey page → submission form → HQ review SLA 7 days. Claims guard: no investment implied, ever.

## Review gates

Weekly 20-min founder review in HQ (Growth + queue). Kill criteria honored: a channel that misses its gate two reviews running gets paused, publicly and without drama — restraint is part of the brand.
