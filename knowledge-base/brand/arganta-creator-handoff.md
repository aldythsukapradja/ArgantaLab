# Arganta Creator — Canonical Handoff

## Purpose

This note is the entry point for Claude, Fable, ChatGPT, designers, editors, and future collaborators working on the **Arganta creator account**.

Arganta is not a generic AI influencer, fictional billionaire, lifelong programmer, or Silicon Valley founder. Arganta is a heightened public expression of **Aldhyt Sukapradja’s real life story**:

> A petroleum geoscientist who spent more than fifteen years modelling complex, invisible systems beneath the earth, then progressively moved through automation, business intelligence, data, machine learning, digital products, and agentic AI—before building Arganta Core and an independent product ecosystem.

The public story should feel human, ambitious, technically credible, and emotionally grounded.

## Canonical One-Sentence Positioning

> I spent fifteen years modelling invisible worlds beneath the earth. Now I build intelligent systems above it.

## Longer Positioning

Arganta follows a real journey beginning in 2010:

1. Petroleum geology education in Indonesia.
2. Basin-scale studies, exploration, petrophysics, and geomodelling.
3. International master’s education in France.
4. Reservoir geology, field development, drilling operations, and giant oil and gas fields.
5. Publications, automation scripts, web tools, and business intelligence.
6. Statistical modelling, machine-learning pilots, and digital products.
7. Expatriate leadership in the Middle East.
8. Agentic AI transformation and product ownership.
9. Arganta Core and the attempt to build several connected products as a solo founder.

## The Correct Mental Model

```text
Geology
→ systems thinking
→ geomodelling
→ automation
→ business intelligence
→ statistical modelling
→ machine learning
→ digital products
→ agentic AI
→ Arganta Core
```

This is a continuous evolution, not an abrupt career change.

## Public Persona

**Name:** Arganta  
**Archetype:** The Systems Builder  
**Alternative campaign title:** The Impossible Builder  
**Primary identity:** Geoscientist, transformation leader, product builder, founder in progress  
**Core tension:** Accomplished expert in one world, beginner-founder in another  
**Emotional promise:** The audience sees both confidence and uncertainty  
**Narrative engine:** Can one domain expert use AI and systems thinking to build what normally requires a company?

## Non-Negotiable Decisions

- Arganta reflects Aldhyt’s real biography and career.
- The account speaks in first person and reads like a real personal creator account.
- Do not lead the Instagram bio with an AI-disclosure statement.
- Do not invent achievements, employers, products, publications, awards, or personal events.
- Do not call the operating intelligence AURA or JARVIS.
- The canonical name is **Arganta Core**.
- The workspace is real and should remain recognisable.
- The visual style may be cinematic, but the evidence must remain authentic.
- Never present simulated data, concept metrics, or placeholders as live.
- Never expose employer-confidential screens, data, numbers, workflows, or identities.
- Arganta is not a computer scientist and should never pretend to be one.
- Arganta did not “leave geology for AI.” AI is an extension of his systems-building journey.
- Avoid direct imitation of Iron Man. Use the emotional energy of a technical inventor, but preserve an original visual language.
