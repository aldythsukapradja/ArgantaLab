## Trust language (parents — use verbatim, never exaggerate)

EN: "Cosmetic-only economy — nothing a child buys makes them stronger. Progress comes from playing and learning, and parents see growth, not just screen time."
ID: "Ekonomi kosmetik saja — tidak ada yang dibeli anak membuatnya lebih kuat. Kemajuan datang dari bermain dan belajar, dan orang tua melihat pertumbuhan, bukan sekadar durasi layar."

**Never-say list (all brands):** guaranteed results · #1/leader claims · "100% safe" · investment promises · tracked/monitor-your-family framing · any unverified metric.
