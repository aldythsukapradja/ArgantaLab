# F5 — Social architecture & content operating system

Absorbs handoff §§14–15. Home fields: `presence.*`, `voice.pillars`, `voice.hashtags`, `content.captionFormula`. This is what the MCP's voice block feeds on.

## Account architecture (restraint is the strategy)

| Phase | Accounts | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Now | **@argantalab (IG, live — preserve)** + founder's personal LinkedIn | one publishing pipeline, one queue |
| +Master | @arganta (IG + LinkedIn page) when www ships | Arganta account cross-posts curated Lab wins |
| Later | @lashirabloom only when the game has a public build; Kinetik Circle markets *through* Arganta + Lab first; **Circle HQ never gets an account** | a product account launches only with 8+ weeks of dedicated content banked |

Cross-promotion: each brand may appear on another's account max 1-in-5 posts, always as its own visual world ("rooms in one universe").

**Kinetik Circle:** The Week Together · Small Family Wins · Rhythm not Rules.

## Caption formula (`content.captionFormula`)

```
HOOK ≤125 chars (curiosity gap or concrete win — the fold line)
↳ BODY 1–3 short lines (one specific thing; show, don't promise)
↳ CTA one line (from voice.ctas)
↳ HASHTAGS 3–5 (branded 1 + category 2 + community 1–2)
Touchy rule: every 3rd post minimum = a REAL artifact (kid creation,
shipped feature, real family moment) — specificity is the warmth.
```

## Cadence (solo-founder honest)

3 posts/week per active account max — Mon build log · Wed product story · Fri ship/win. Stories ad-hoc. A missed day is silence, never filler. Pause rule: if queue < 1 week banked, drop to 2/week rather than lower quality.
