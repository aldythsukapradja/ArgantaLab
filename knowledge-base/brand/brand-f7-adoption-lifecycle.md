# F7 — Adoption, onboarding & lifecycle copy

Absorbs handoff §§18–19. Activation events feed Growth analytics event names; key strings land per-surface. All definitions `working-hypothesis` until measured.

## Activation events (validated + refined from the handoff)

| Product | Activation (the moment it "took") | Refinement vs handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Arganta | visitor starts the Build/Partner journey **or follows a product link through** | added the follow-through — the gateway's job is routing |
| ArgantaLab | child ships a first *visible* artifact (KinQuest win, drill streak, or Builder publish) **and sees the reward land** | "meaningful activity" sharpened to *artifact + reward seen* |
| Kinetik Circle | circle created/joined **+ first plan that a second member actually sees** | one-person circles aren't activation |
| LashiraBloom | first grow-and-harvest cycle **within a family circle** | solo harvest ≠ the family promise |
| Circle HQ | founder reviews a real signal and records a decision | confirmed as-is |

## Lifecycle copy principles

First-run promises one thing and delivers it inside 2 minutes. Empty states are invitations with a verb, never apologies. Progress celebrates the *artifact* ("your first game is live"), not the metric ("7-day streak!"). Notifications: max 1/day per product, family-quiet hours default 20:30–07:00, every one deep-links to the thing itself. Win-back never guilts — it shows what grew while you were away (Bloom literally: "your pumpkins came in").

## Key strings (EN / ID)

**Welcome (Lab):** "Welcome to the Lab. Build your first thing today — Buddy's waiting." / "Selamat datang di Lab. Bikin karya pertamamu hari ini — Buddy sudah menunggu."
**First ship (Lab):** "YOU built that. It's real, it's yours, it's shipped. 🚀" / "Kamu yang bikin ini. Nyata, punyamu, sudah rilis. 🚀"
**Circle invite:** "{{name}} wants your week in one place. Join the circle — it takes a minute." / "{{name}} mengajakmu menyatukan minggu keluarga. Gabung — cuma semenit."
**Bloom return:** "The farm missed you. Two seeds are ready. 🌾" / "Ladangmu kangen. Dua bibit siap panen. 🌾"
**Parent consent (Lab):** "Your child wants to publish '{{title}}' to the family circle. Review it first — you always see what ships." / "Anakmu ingin merilis '{{title}}' ke lingkaran keluarga. Tinjau dulu — kamu selalu melihat apa yang dirilis."
**Error (all, adult):** "That didn't save. Your work is safe — try again." / "Belum tersimpan. Karyamu aman — coba lagi."
**Deletion ack (all):** "Done. Your data is gone from our side. The door stays open." / "Selesai. Datamu sudah dihapus dari sisi kami. Pintu selalu terbuka."

## Email/notification frequency contract

Transactional: always. Weekly family summary (Circle): opt-out-able, Sunday evening. Product updates: ≤2/month. Re-engagement: one at day 7, one at day 30, then silence — respect is retention.
