# F9 — The Arganta Marketing Doctrine

Absorbs the 2026-07-17 ChatGPT deep-research handoff ("Philosophical Marketing"). This is the layer **above** F1–F8: F1 says what Arganta believes, F2 says who it serves, F3–F4 say how it sounds — F9 says **why those choices are right, and what governs every external word** across Instagram, decks, websites, product copy and investor material. Provenance discipline applies: every claim below is tagged `source-grounded`, `strategic-inference`, or `founder-locked`. Nothing simulated wears measured's clothes.

## 0 · Research verdict on the trigger insight

The seed Instagram carousel ("wealthy people → time, entrepreneurs → freedom, parents → peace of mind, everyone → no one") is an **unattributable, endlessly reposted meme** — evidence class C. Search across the reposts finds no identifiable original author; the format itself (sepia eagle, capitalized outcomes) is another creator's visual identity and must not be copied. `source-grounded`

But the **mechanism** underneath it is real and well-supported:

- It is compressed **Jobs to Be Done** (Christensen/Moesta): people hire products to make progress in a circumstance, not to own features. Class A.
- It is compressed **outcome positioning** (April Dunford, *Obviously Awesome*): position against the alternative in the buyer's life, in the buyer's words. Class A.
- Its memorability is **compression + specificity + contrast** (Rory Sutherland's behavioral framing; Berger's *Contagious*): a specific audience paired with one surprising word out-travels any paragraph. Class B.
- Its failure mode is **stereotype**: "parents → peace of mind" is true of the purchase moment and false of the relationship. Emotional outcomes differ within a demographic by life stage, culture and risk tolerance. The fix is the claims-discipline ladder (§6): a compressed outcome may open a post, but the proof underneath must be specific and real.

**Adopted rule (founder-locked): Arganta keeps the mechanism — *specific audience → unresolved tension → hidden desire → memorable outcome* — and rejects the artwork, the wording and the pretense of attribution.**

## 1 · The central human truth

> **People are drowning in technology that consumes their attention and starving for technology that returns evidence of their growth.**

Grounded in:
- **Self-Determination Theory** (Deci & Ryan; class A): humans are intrinsically motivated by autonomy, competence and relatedness. Arganta's loop is SDT made product: *Play* (autonomy) → *Learn* (competence) → *Build* (competence made visible) → *Ship* (relatedness — someone sees it). "Grow together" is relatedness stated as a promise.
- **The progress principle** (Amabile; class A): the single strongest motivator in real work-diaries is *visible progress in meaningful work*. Arganta's entire surface language — build logs, readiness percentages, shipped features, growth a parent can see — is progress made legible.
- **Learning-through-play research** (LEGO Foundation lineage, games-based-learning SDT studies; class B): play that supports autonomy, competence and relatedness turns the content itself into the motivation — the opposite of "disguised homework."

**The Arganta belief that follows (founder-locked):**

> **Technology should return agency, make growth visible, and give families something to build instead of something to watch. AI should help good judgment travel further — never replace it.**

## 2 · The one problem beneath the five products

Every Arganta product answers the same tension in a different room of the same house — **invisible effort**:

- A kid's screen time is invisible effort until ArgantaLab turns it into a shipped thing.
- A household's coordination is invisible effort until Kinetik Circle turns it into rhythm.
- A family's time together is invisible effort until LashiraBloom turns it into a world that visibly grows.
- A founder's complexity is invisible effort until Circle HQ turns it into a legible cockpit.
- Fifteen years of subsurface judgment was invisible effort until Arganta made it a company built in the open.

**This is the pitch-deck spine and the answer to "why is this one company, not five side projects": one mechanism — *make invisible effort visible* — applied to childhood, household, family, and founder.** `strategic-inference`, validated against every shipped surface.

## 4 · The persona — The Systems Builder, validated

The handoff's working archetype survives the challenge, with one refinement: the differentiation is not "builds systems" (every founder claims that) — it is the **subsurface metaphor**, which no other founder in this space can honestly use. `strategic-inference`

> **A geoscientist who spent fifteen years modeling worlds no one can see now builds systems that make invisible growth visible.**

- **Archetype:** Creator (builder) with Explorer secondary. Not Sage (no lecturing), not Ruler (no empire talk), not Caregiver-as-primary (the products carry the care; the persona carries the craft).
- **Core belief:** incomplete evidence, honestly modeled, beats confident fiction — in reservoirs and in companies.
- **Enemy / rejected worldview:** attention-harvesting technology; effortless-success theatre; AI as replacement.
- **Human tension (what makes it credible):** a father building family products while learning founding in public — the uncertainty is shown, never performed.
- **Refuses:** guru posture, invented traction, children's faces, fear-based parent marketing, borrowed visual identities, "revolutionizing" vocabulary (full list in F3 never-say).
- **Facelessness strategy:** the recurring visual object is the **cross-section / strata / contour** — the geologist's way of showing what's beneath. Ownable, honest, and unclaimed by any creator in the reference list.
- **Signature line (hypothesis to A/B, not final bio):** *"I spent fifteen years modelling worlds underground. Now I build systems that help families grow above them."*

## 6 · Claims discipline (the anti-manipulation mechanism)

Every external sentence is classifiable, and the class dictates the verb tense:

1. **belief** — present tense, first person, never sourced to fake research
2. **aspiration** — future tense, explicitly ("we're building toward…")
3. **current capability** — present tense, only if repo-verified
4. **demonstrated evidence** — past tense with the artifact linked
5. **customer outcome** — only with a real, consenting example
6. **hypothesis** — labeled as such, even on Instagram

**Never write class 2 in class 3's tense.** This single rule is what separates Arganta's emotional marketing from the manipulative kind the handoff's §6.3 warns about — the emotion is real because the proof is real. `founder-locked`

## 8 · The ten doctrine principles

1. **Sell the visible growth, never the app.** The product disappears; the transformation is the message — with the proof attached.
2. **One audience, one tension, one word.** Every piece of external communication picks a single row of the desire map.
3. **The build log is the pitch.** (Method Law 17, elevated to marketing law.)
4. **Compression opens, evidence closes.** A memorable line may start the post; a real artifact must end it.
5. **Never rent fear.** Parental guilt, expert obsolescence and founder FOMO are tensions to *resolve*, never to inflame.
6. **The metaphor is subsurface.** Strata, contours, cross-sections, hidden layers made visible — the one visual territory that is authentically Aldyth's.
7. **The persona is a builder, not a guru.** Share the method and the failure; never sell the dream.
8. **One mind, many rooms.** Instagram, deck, website and UI differ in temperature (§7), never in worldview.
9. **AI is the named co-builder.** Never hidden, never the hero.
10. **When in doubt, ship the thing and let it speak.** Silence over nonsense (Method Law 18) applies to marketing too.
