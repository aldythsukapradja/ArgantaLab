# F1 — Brand foundation & architecture

Absorbs handoff §§01–03. Provenance: `founder-locked` unless marked. Home fields: `voice.boilerplates`, `routing.siteUrl`, endorsement rules → F3/F5.

## Why Arganta exists

**Belief:** families grow best when they build together — and ideas, like children, deserve a beginning someone believes in.

**Purpose:** give families and founders one place where playing, learning, planning and building feed each other instead of competing for attention.

**Mission:** ship a connected family universe — play that teaches, tools that create, rhythms that hold a household together — built in the open by a founder and an AI co-builder.

**Master promise:** **Grow together.** / ID: **Tumbuh bersama.**

**Emotional territory:** warm competence. The feeling of a workshop at night: something real got made today, and the family saw it happen.

## Values → behaviours

| Value | It looks like | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Show, don't promise | posting the build log, not the roadmap | fake traction, "coming soon" theatre |
| Earn the kid's trust, keep the parent's | cosmetic-only economy, visible learning | pay-to-win, dark patterns, surveillance framing |
| One universe | shared Kin, shared economy, shared design bones | five disconnected apps |
| Honest provenance | measured vs simulated always labeled | inflated claims anywhere |

## Architecture (endorsed-brand structure)

```
ARGANTA — masterbrand + external gateway (www.arganta.app)
├─ for families/kids:  ArgantaLab · Kinetik Circle · LashiraBloom   ("Part of Arganta")
├─ for founders:       Build with Arganta · Arganta Spark|Seed (TBD)
└─ internal:           Circle HQ (hq.arganta.app — never marketed publicly)
Kingdom: parked engine donor · Bridge/apps/landing: infrastructure, not a brand
```

**Endorsement rules:** product logo leads on its own surfaces; "Part of Arganta — the trusted family OS" appears in bios and About pages; the Arganta mark leads only on www, press and partnership materials. "by Arganta" lockup required on app-store listings; never stack two product logos side by side without the master.

**Naming (public):** Arganta · ArgantaLab · **Kinetik Circle** (space) · LashiraBloom (one word, capital B) · **Circle HQ** (space). Never publicly: Landing, The Bridge, Kingdom-as-flagship, CircleHQ, Lashira Bloom. Code ids stay: `argantalab`, `kinetikcircle`, `lashirabloom`, `circlehq`, `arganta`.

**Domains:** www.arganta.app (canonical, root redirects) · lab. · circle. · bloom. · hq. — all `requires-validation` until DNS verified in the matrix.
